1. Product overview, goals and scope
1.1 What we are building
A city-by-category classifieds platform for India. Anyone can browse listings for free. Registered, verified advertisers ("vendors") post listings. Posting is free but limited. Vendors pay to push listings to the top of the list during the hours buyers browse, using a prepaid credits wallet. Buyers contact vendors directly by phone, WhatsApp, Telegram or email; the platform takes no commission on deals.
1.2 Business goals
Win Google traffic for every "category + city" and "category + district" search through an automatically generated page grid.
Fill the site with listings through free posting, then convert active vendors to paid visibility products.
Earn revenue from: paid promotions (Top, Auto Boost, Flash, Star), upgrades (Super Top, Highlight, New label), Publishing Packs, a vendor subscription plan, and paid add-ons (Verified badge, contact-limit unlock).
Keep buyers returning through a feed that changes all day (driven by paid bumps), plus saved contacts.
1.3 In scope (v1)
Public site (User Panel): home, category, state, city, district listing pages, search, filters, ad detail page, contact, save contact, report ad, buyer account.
Vendor Panel: registration, email + phone + KYC verification, dashboard, 3-step posting wizard, media albums, ad management, promotions, wallet, coupons, Publishing Packs, subscription, auto-renewal, analytics, settings.
Admin Panel: full control of users, vendors, ads, media, moderation, KYC, catalogue (categories, attributes, locations), products and prices, slots, coupons, packs, subscriptions, finance, SEO, CMS, help centre, blog, templates, roles, audit logs, feature flags.
Support: help centre, ticketing, WhatsApp/Telegram support desk, scam reporting site, customer code verification.
SEO engine, payments, notifications (email, SMS, WhatsApp), tracking.
Fully responsive on mobile, tablet and desktop for all three panels.
1.4 Out of scope (v1)
Native iOS/Android apps (the reference platform has none; mobile web covers it). An installable web app (PWA) is listed as P1 in section 5.
In-site buyer-vendor chat (contact happens off-platform, as on the reference). Listed as an open decision.
Taking payment for vendors&apos; services, escrow or commissions.
Cryptocurrency payments (present on the reference; kept as a feature flag, default off — see section 12).

5.4.1 Feed ranking rules (P0)
Star ads for the current city and slot are shown in their own strip, never more than the slot capacity (default 6).
All other live ads are sorted by last_bumped_at, newest first. A free ad&apos;s last_bumped_at = its publish time. Every paid bump sets last_bumped_at = bump time, which moves the ad to position 1.
Ties are broken by promotion level (Super Top > Top > free), then by ad ID.
Expired, suspended, deleted, blocked or pending-moderation ads never appear.

7.1.1 Step 1 checks (P0)
Banned-word filter: on submit, scan title and description against the banned-word list; block with a message naming the exact word. Admin can also set words to "flag for review" instead of block.
Duplicate detection: if title/description is identical or very similar to a recent ad (similarity threshold configurable), block with "this text matches an existing listing; please change it before publishing".
AI rewrite: on a duplicate block, offer "Generate with AI". It asks for/uses the vendor&apos;s phone number to read their previous ads and generates new text; Regenerate up to 3 times (counter "0/3"); vendor can edit; "Use the AI text" fills the fields.
Weekly limit check: if the free weekly limit is reached, show a weekly-limit message with options: promote the ad, buy a Publishing Pack (when enabled), or save as draft.
7.2 Step 2 — Photos and media (P0)
Upload by drag-and-drop or file picker; multiple files at once; upload in groups recommended; progress bar.
Pick from the vendor&apos;s media albums (section 8) or upload new files into an album.
Drag to reorder; choose a cover photo (if none chosen, the first photo becomes the cover).
Shows how many photos will be visible: free/Basic ads show 1 (with a prompt explaining that promoting unlocks more photos); promoted ads show up to 10.
Grading result shown per photo (section 8.2). A rejected photo cannot be used. Restricted photos cannot be the cover.
Buttons: Save and continue, Continue without photo (warning that the listing will have no cover image), Save as draft.
Video (P1): add videos from the media library; choose photo or video as preview.
Photo guidelines link and tips on how photos improve contacts.
7.3 Step 3 — Promote or publish (P0)
Shows an ad preview (desktop and mobile), then two paths:
Path A — Promote (instant go-live)
Choose a product: Top, Flash or Star (cards showing benefits; "Learn how it works" links).
For Top: choose duration (1, 3 or 7 days), bumps per day (3 or 6), time slot (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night — 6 hours each), and optional Auto Boost (10 bumps inside one 6-hour slot).
For Star: see the availability calendar for the next 7 days (today + 6) for slots 10:00–22:00 and 22:00–10:00; pick one or more slots (running count of selected slots); sold-out slots greyed out with a sold-out message. Needs at least 1 Safe photo and a display name. Credits only.
For Flash: starts now, runs 4 hours.
Add upgrades: Super Top, Highlight, New label, or All Extras (bundle discount shown). Upgrades last as long as the Top.
Show the price for slot purchase vs hourly purchase, with the % saving.
Subscriber benefits applied automatically (+1 free bump per day, discounted Super Top).
Optional Auto-renewal toggle (section 11.6).
Basket: items, credits or rupee total, remove item (with a confirmation prompt), apply coupon.
Mandatory consent tick-box: the vendor agrees to immediate activation and accepts that the cancellation/withdrawal right ends once the service starts.
Pay: with credits (instant checkout, no payment details needed) or direct payment (card/UPI etc., section 12). A billing address is required before the first purchase.
On success: ad goes live instantly; bump schedule created and shown in a calendar; confirmation screen with "Manage ad".
Path B — Publish as Basic Ad (free or Publishing Pack)
Uses the weekly free allowance (default 2 per week, Monday–Sunday) or, when Publishing Packs are enabled, one Basic Ad from the pack balance.
Goes to the moderation queue; with a message that free listings are reviewed before going live. Vendor sees status "Pending review".
Only 1 photo visible; contact cap applies.
Reminder text: number of free ads left this week for this phone number.
If Basic publishing is unavailable (limit reached, pack needed), show a message offering to promote now or try later, with a link to buy a Publishing Pack.
Path C — Save as draft
Drafts are listed in Ads › Drafts and can be published later .
7.4 Errors and edge cases (P0)
Payment failed / interrupted → basket kept, ad stays unpublished, clear reason (see 12.6).
Top bought inside or too close to the chosen slot → first bump moves to the next day; tell the vendor before payment.
Session timeout → autosave the draft every step.
Generic publish-failure message with retry and support link.
8. Vendor Panel — media albums and photo grading
8.1 Media library and albums (P0)
Media section in the Vendor Panel: create albums, rename, delete; upload photos (and videos, P1) into albums.
Every file is checked once on upload (automatic grading + optional human review) and can then be reused across many ads.
Approved media do not appear in ads automatically; the vendor selects them when creating or editing an ad.
Album limits (count of albums, files per album) configurable; a limit increase can be granted when previously rejected files are later approved.
Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC (photos); MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WEBM (videos, max length and size configurable; first N seconds auto-trimmed option).
Automatic compression, resizing and WebP versions for speed; EXIF location stripped for privacy.

Optional "Verify photos" flow: photos are matched against the vendor&apos;s KYC data ("digitally matched"); flagged as optional now with a configurable date when it becomes mandatory.

Paid Verified badge add-on (section 10.6) highlights verification on cards and in search.

10.3 Scheduler and bump engine (P0)
When a promotion is paid, the system generates the full bump schedule (date-times) for its duration, bumps per day and slot, spreading bumps evenly in the slot (Auto Boost: 10 bumps in the slot; Flash: 5 bumps in 60 minutes). Schedule is shown to the vendor in a calendar.
A job runner executes bumps on time (to the minute), sets last_bumped_at, and logs every bump. Missed bumps (outage) are re-run or compensated (admin setting).
Cut-off rule: if a purchase is made inside the chosen slot, or less than X hours before it (default 2 hours, configurable), the first bump day moves to the next day. Nothing is back-dated. The vendor is warned before payment.

Slot purchase vs hourly purchase: show the % saving of buying a full slot.

14. Moderation, trust and safety
14.1 Moderation model (P0)
Basic (free) ads: pre-moderation — they wait in a queue before going live.
Promoted ads: post-moderation — they go live instantly and are reviewed afterwards. Speed is part of what vendors pay for.
Edits to live ads: re-checked automatically; risky edits go to the queue.
Rule-breaking ads found later are removed with a reason; vendor notified; repeat offences lead to account warnings, suspension or ban.
14.2 Automatic checks (P0)
Banned-word list (block or flag), applied on post and on every list update (live ads re-scanned).
Duplicate text detection with AI rewrite offer.
Image grading and duplicate/stolen image detection.
Phone/email/URL detection inside descriptions and images (configurable rule).
KYC-verified vendors only; phone verification; email verification.
Bot protection, rate limits, VPN/IP rules, disposable email block.
Screening for adult/escort or other prohibited content disguised as listings (keywords + image moderation) — protects the payment gateway and hosting accounts.
14.3 Reports (P0)
Report an ad (public form on every ad): reason list (fraud/scam, wrong category, fake photos, already sold/rented, offensive, prohibited, other), details, screenshot upload, reporter email.
Submit a report page (general).
Scam report flow (separate page/site): asks for at least one screenshot as proof and a description; fast-track queue.
A report never removes an ad automatically; moderators decide. Reporter gets an acknowledgement and outcome email.
14.4 Scam education and official-message verification (P0)
Safety section in the help centre (section 16.2 lists the articles).
Rule: official WhatsApp/SMS/email messages always include the user&apos;s Customer Code; help article explains how to check.
Rule: staff never ask for money outside the platform; the platform never takes payment for vendors&apos; services.
Security tip tile on the vendor dashboard and safety box on every ad page.
14.5 Legal takedowns and grievances (P0, India)
Grievance Officer page with name, contact and process.
Complaint intake: acknowledge within 24 hours, resolve within the legally required time (currently reported as 7 days).
Government/court orders: takedown within the legally required time (currently reported as 3 hours); nudity/sexual-content complaints within 2 hours. On-call admin alerting.
Takedown log: what, when, by whom, legal basis, evidence kept as required.

OWASP Top 10 protection; CSRF tokens on all forms; parameterised queries; output escaping.
WAF + bot protection (e.g. Cloudflare); rate limits on login, OTP, posting, contact reveals and reports.
2FA for all admin users; strong password policy; password expiry for vendors.
Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for personal data and KYC files; KYC documents in restricted storage with masked display.
Payment card data never touches our servers (gateway-hosted fields).
Secrets in a secret manager; never in code or repos.
Regular backups (daily, 30-day retention) and tested restore.
21.4 Privacy (P0)
Comply with India&apos;s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023: consent records, purpose limits, data access and deletion on request, breach notification process, data retention rules, privacy policy and cookie policy pages.
21.5 Availability and monitoring (P0)
99.9% monthly uptime target for public pages and checkout; uptime, error and job monitoring; alerting to on-call staff; status page (P1).
21.6 Accessibility (P1)
WCAG 2.1 AA for public pages: contrast, keyboard navigation, alt texts, form labels.
21.7 Languages (P1)
English at launch; Gujarati and Hindi next. All strings from the translation module; per-language SEO templates and help/blog content.
21.8 Reference technology (for information only)
The researched platform appears to use: Django (Python) back-end, MongoDB, server-rendered Bootstrap front-end, Cloudflare (CDN, WAF, bot check), Zendesk (help centre), PostHog + Google Tag Manager/GA (tracking), and Incode (KYC). The development team chooses the final stack; any modern stack that meets this PRD is acceptable.
22. Legal and compliance (India)
Summary of research, not legal advice. Confirm with an Indian lawyer and CA before launch.
Intermediary duties (IT Act s.79 + IT Rules 2021 and amendments): terms that prohibit unlawful, obscene and misleading content; a named Grievance Officer resident in India; complaint acknowledgement in 24 hours and resolution within the required time (reported as 7 days); takedown of government/court-ordered content within the required time (reported as 3 hours); nudity complaints within 2 hours; records of takedowns.
Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020: clear seller details, grievance mechanism, no misleading listings, price transparency.
DPDP Act 2023: consent, privacy notice, data rights, KYC data handling.
GST: tax invoices for credits, products and subscriptions; GST registration.
Payment gateway policies: prohibited content screening, refund and cancellation policy pages, terms, privacy, contact page — needed for gateway approval.
Prohibited content: an admin-managed prohibited-content list in the terms, actively screened (sections 8 and 14). Any content involving minors is absolutely prohibited and reported to authorities.
Applicable content laws: see the R&D report, section 13 (IT Act s.67/67A, BNS s.294, ITPA). Get legal advice before launch.
