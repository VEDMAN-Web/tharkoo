# H19.10: Video call earnings, vendor 70 percent and Tharkoo 30 percent

## Done (server, all with tests in tests/server/tours/h19-10-split.test.ts, 20 tests)
1. Money setting "Tharkoo share of video calls (%)", default 30, whole 0 to 90, in Admin, Prices, Money settings (src/app/admin/prices/_view/MoneyCard.tsx). Saved through the existing audited money save; a superadmin only (an admin gets "Only a superadmin changes the share of video calls").
2. `requestTour` stores the price and the share in force (`feePercent`) on the call. A later change never touches it. Calls made before this change have no share (0), so they pay the vendor everything, as before.
3. A paid call (both connected, `endTour` paid): vendor gets floor(price x (100 - share) / 100), Tharkoo the rest to platform_revenue. 100 at 30 = 70 / 30; 15 at 30 = 10 / 5; 15 at 0 = 15 / 0. Trial balance 0 in every test. A zero vendor part is skipped in the ledger (it cannot happen from price 10).
4. Refunds (declined, expired, call too short or failed) give the whole price back, no fee.
5. `reverseTour` takes the vendor part back from vendor_earnings and the fee back from platform_revenue and returns the full price to the user's call credits; trial balance 0.
6. Lowest call price 10 credits: the video tours admin refuses less (screen minimum 10) and a category saved without a new price is lifted to 10. One-off migration `raiseLowCallPrices` (src/server/tours/min-price.ts): runs on the first video call request and is safe to repeat; it raises every category under 10 (or with no price, which meant 1) to 10 and writes one audit row "video_tours.min_price.raise" listing each category and its old price. Nothing is run here; the log will be on live after the first call request. The H19.8 form is not built yet: it must use TOUR_MIN_PRICE_COINS from src/server/tours/split.ts.
