# H8.49 (A49): global search

## Done
- The top bar search of the admin now works. The "/" key (or the Search button on a phone) opens it; typing 2 or more characters shows grouped results under the field: Go to (screens this role can open), Ads, Users, Support tickets, Orders & credits, Pages and content. Arrow keys move, Enter opens the row, Esc closes and clears. Each row is a link to the right screen (the ad, the user drawer, the ticket, the Orders list for that payment reference, the page lists).
- `src/server/admin/search.ts` and `GET /api/admin/search` (staff only, never cached). Each group is searched only when the role has the permission for that screen: ads (ads.view or ads.review), users (users.view), tickets (desk.work), orders (orders.view, finance), pages and content (content.write or pages.edit). A group with no result is left out. Support therefore never gets an Orders row, from the screen or from the API.
- A query is trimmed, must be 2 or more characters, is cut at 80, and is always matched as plain text (special characters are escaped). 5 rows per group.
- On a phone the results are a full-width sheet under the field; rows are 44 px tall.

## Not done
- Search over "content" means titles of pages, blog posts and help articles for now (the Content studio is not built); rows open the existing list screens.
- Ads are found by title, slug and ID; the ticket by number or subject; payments by reference prefix. No full text search.
