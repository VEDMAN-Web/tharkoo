## Needs follow-up

- `POST /api/saved/{id}`, recently viewed, alerts, analytics, notifications, subscription, packs, media library, account deletion, profile edit, and bank transfer have no API. Those screens say coming soon.
- Photo upload on the post wizard is coming soon. Submit still calls the listings API.

- Legacy listing rows from `queryListings` do not include a Mongo id. Reveal looks up `GET /api/listings/{slug}` first.
- CMS pages such as about are not in the seed. `/cms/about/` says the page is not published yet.
- `graphify` is not installed in this environment. `graphify update .` was not run. `graphify-out/` was not created.
- T8.1 ownership copies every T5.1 glob so this branch still passes the check against `main`. Drop those globs when T5.1 merges.
- `src/app/favicon.ico` is not in the T8.1 globs.
- Route `loading.tsx` files streamed the canonical and description into the body, which failed the SEO audit. The home feed still streams behind a Suspense boundary. The shell data is awaited in the root layout so metadata stays in `<head>`.
- Pre-existing identifiers in server code contain the letters "Inter" (`InternalError`, and one seed sentence). Those files are not part of this edit.

## Round 2

Re-audit UI_AUDIT_v2 on the same branch. Not merged and not deployed.

- P0 staging sign-in: `STAGING_AUTO_VERIFY_EMAIL=true` marks the email verified at register only when `SITE_NOINDEX=true`. Any other combination is refused. Documented in `.env.example`. With no SMS provider, the phone OTP is logged server-side and is not returned in production. The console mailer logs the message body when `SITE_NOINDEX=true` or the process is not production.
- Photos: six Unsplash webp files per category, about 800px, plus a blur sibling and a home hero, under `public/placeholders/`. Sources and the Unsplash licence are in `public/placeholders/CREDITS.md`. No faces. Seed assigns 1–4 photos per demo listing and refreshes existing `demo-*` rows. Dates are 1–30 days old. Demo contacts are fictional `+9198000…` numbers stored on the contact field.
- Home: hero (headline, lead, search, city pill, counts, preloaded skyline) and a 1/2/3/4 image card grid. Gold is the accent. Buttons are outline.
- Search matches tokens across title, description, category, and city, with flat/apartment/rent and job/hiring/vacancy. An empty query says `No results for “…”` and offers categories.
- Reveal shows the seeded number after the click and keeps it out of the first HTML. A missing contact says the poster has not shared one. Labels stay on one line.
- City choice wins over the URL, on desktop and mobile, and the pill truncates the full name.
- Age gate is a centred blocking dialog. The cookie banner opens only after it.
- Admin pages drop the public header. Dashboard tiles are dark and count active listings, city locations, and ads with a phone.
- Post-ad contact step hints at `+91` and a Telegram username, and rejects a bad format.
- Logged-out account screens skip wallet, listings, and profile fetches unless the `tk-in` cookie is set.
- Public pages emit WebPage JSON-LD. Nearby renders only with 3 or more items. About, terms, privacy, and cookies have their own titles. KYC and Settings no longer say coming soon.
