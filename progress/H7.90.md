# H7.90: compact search results page (part 1)

## Done
- /search/ has a compact results header instead of the 400 px photo band: breadcrumb, an H1 built from the filters ("All ads in India", "Ads in Surat", "Dating ads in Gujarat", "Results for “yoga”"), the number of ads, a Sort select (Best match when there are words, Newest, Online now first, Verified first), and a row of active filter chips with a remove cross each and Clear all. Category, city, district and tag pages keep their photo band.
- The keyword box is the first field of the filter column (label "Keyword (optional)"), with suggestions from category names, city names and ad titles (200 ms pause, at most six, arrow keys, Enter, Escape) from the new /api/search/suggest.
- queryListings can put ads of verified sellers, or of sellers online now, first (prefer), inside the chosen order.
