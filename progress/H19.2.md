# H19.2: Ad page

## Done
1. Guests: the contact buttons were already the same for guests and signed-in visitors (the page is cached, so it cannot differ). Checked with a new e2e: a guest sees Call, WhatsApp and Chat (and Video call when the ad may have calls), and Chat opens the sign-in step in a pop-up on the same ad; after the code the chat or video pop-up continues on that ad (the pop-up signs in without leaving the page, so the visitor is on the same ad by construction). Call and WhatsApp need no sign-in today and still do not.
2. The sample-ad box ("This is a sample ad ...", Post an ad) is removed. The Sample ad label stays.
3. Breadcrumb above the photo: Tharkoo India / City / Area (Area only when the ad has one). The category is not in it. The page markup (JSON-LD) keeps its full SEO crumbs from the route.
4. Similar ads near you: four cards in a 2 x 2 grid on phone and desktop (no carousel, no arrows). They are the Home-style cards, with views and clicks.
5. Vendor on their own ad: "This is your ad" with Edit (opens the ad in the post form) and Boost (opens Promote); no Call, WhatsApp, Chat or Video call. Because the ad page is cached, the owner is found in the browser: the page now carries the vendor's id (`ownerId`) and the contact buttons compare it with the signed-in user.
