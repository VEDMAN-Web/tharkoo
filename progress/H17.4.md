# H17.4: local links (Opus #412)

## Before
- "Nearby" under a city page listed other cities that have ads in the same category from ANY state (Surat showed Bhopal and others), then other categories of the same city.
- "Popular searches" were the fixed tag pages of the category, joined to the category or area name, which made phrases that no one searches and some pages that do not fit ("Property Lesson").

## Now (src/lib/seo/local-links.ts, used by the city, district and category pages)
- Nearby (city page): cities of the SAME state only, never the city itself, the ones with the most live ads in this category first, then by name, at most 5. A state with no other city shows nothing. The site stores no coordinates for cities, so there is no distance step: same state is the whole rule, so a far-away state never appears.
- Popular searches: only real category + city pages that have ads (the biggest first, one per city, never the page being shown). On the category page they are other categories' pages (the category's own cities are in its Nearby list); on a district page, at most 4. Each is named "Category in City" from the site's own names. The tag pages themselves are unchanged and still open; they are no longer used as made-up phrases.
