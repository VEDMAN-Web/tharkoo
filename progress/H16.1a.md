# H16.1a: India cities and areas data files, and the CSV reader (part 1 of H16.1)

Part 1 of 3: the data and the reader. Part 2 (H16.1b) is the admin "Import cities and areas (CSV)" with preview and Undo. Part 3 (H16.1c) is the location pickers for about 900 cities. Nothing here changes the site by itself: no code reads these files yet.

## What is in the PR
- `data/locations/india-cities.csv`: 886 rows. Columns: name, state, slug, isDistrictHq, population, source.
  - 757 rows are district headquarters, 385 rows carry a population, every row has a source address.
  - Every state and union territory has at least one row (the test checks this). State names are the exact names of the site's state rows.
  - Web addresses (slug) follow the site's own rules and are unique in the whole file, because a city address on Tharkoo has no state in it. When two places share a name (Aurangabad in Bihar, Bijapur in Chhattisgarh, Bilaspur in Himachal Pradesh, Udaipur in Tripura, Balrampur, Hamirpur and Pratapgarh in Uttar Pradesh, Bishnupur in West Bengal) the larger or older one keeps the plain address and the other has the state added, like `aurangabad-bihar`. The cities the site already has (59) keep their spelling and address (Gurgaon, Mysuru, Bengaluru and so on).
- `data/locations/india-areas.csv`: 564 rows for 19 cities (10 to 40 areas each). Columns: city (the city's web address), name, slug, source.
- `src/server/locations/import-csv.ts`: reads a CSV (quotes, commas inside quotes, CRLF, BOM), checks the header, cleans every cell, and finds the problems that need no database (empty or long name, markup, bad web address). Part 2 uses it.
- `tests/server/locations/h16-1-csv.test.ts`: 8 tests for the reader and the two data files.

## Where the data comes from (public, neutral data only; no competitor site was visited or copied)
- Cities of 1 lakh and more with 2011 Census populations: the Wikipedia list "List of cities in India by population" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_India_by_population), 339 rows. The population is the number printed there, nothing is estimated.
- District headquarters: Wikidata (https://www.wikidata.org), the "headquarters" of each district of India; the row's source is the Wikidata page of that place.
- Other towns of 1 lakh and more: Wikidata places with a population of 100,000 or more; the row's source is the Wikidata page and the population is the one it states.
- Three capitals the lists leave out but the site already has (Itanagar, Port Blair, Kavaratti): their Wikipedia pages.
- Areas: the Wikipedia category "Neighbourhoods in <city>" (the row's source is that category page), plus Wikidata neighbourhoods where it has them. For each city the 40 areas whose article is longest are kept (a plain proxy for "well known"). Names that are plainly not areas (stations, roads, parks, colleges, forests) are dropped.
- Licence: Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA and Wikidata is CC0. The names and numbers are facts; the source column keeps the attribution.

## Known gaps (nothing was invented to fill them)
- Areas: 11 of the 30 largest cities have fewer than 10 areas in the public sources, so they are NOT in the areas file: Jaipur (4), Nagpur (3), Ghaziabad (6), Varanasi (4), Meerut (1), Vadodara, Ludhiana, Nashik, Faridabad, Rajkot and Aurangabad (none found). They need another source or a hand-written list.
- District headquarters: 30 districts have no headquarters in Wikidata (most are districts made in 2022 to 2024): Zanskar, Sankoo, Changthang, Drass, Nubra and Sham (Ladakh); Surguja, Mohla-Manpur-Ambagarh Chowki and Khairagarh-Chhuikhadan-Gandai (Chhattisgarh); Longding, Lower Siang, Bichom, Keyi Panyor, Kamle and Itanagar Capital Complex (Arunachal Pradesh); Niuland, Shamator and Meluri (Nagaland); Tamulpur (Assam); Hansi (Haryana); Malegaon (Maharashtra); Kushavati (Goa); Chandigarh and Lakshadweep (their capitals are in the file); and some Delhi revenue districts, which are parts of one city. A state or union territory still always has at least one city in the file.
- Where the same place came from more than one list (same state, same web address), the rows were merged into one and all sources are kept in the source column.
- Delhi's district headquarters are neighbourhoods of one city, so they are left out of the cities file.
