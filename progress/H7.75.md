# H7.75: search and every listing page share one body

## Done
- /search/ (with or without words, state, city, area, category, filters) now renders in the same frame as the category page: the title band, the left filter column (Category, State, City, Area, quick filters, the category's own filters, Clear all and Search), the list at the same width, 15 ads per page, the shared pager with "Showing a-b of N ads", and the fallback ads. It stays noindex. The place selects stay on /search/ and keep the other picks; the filter form carries the picks as hidden fields.
- Tag pages and district pages use the same frame and filter column, and the quick filters narrow their lists. Sub-category hub pages use it too, with the place selects only (their list has no quick filters).
- The old listing body is removed: ListingPageView no longer has a plain (non-frame) mode, its own header and filter blocks, or the crumb menu; the props that only fed them are gone.
- searchListings takes the area, video, online and the category field picks.

## Not done
- Phone band alignment, the empty gap and the Filters button (item B of the same request) come in the next PR.
