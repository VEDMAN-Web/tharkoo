# H8.26 (A26): History & undo screen

## Done
- `/admin/history/` is built (it was a "Coming" stub). Tabs: Recent changes and Full audit log (admins only). Search, Who, Type and When filters, the "Only what I can undo" button, a Previous/Next table with 25, 50 or 100 per page, and an Export link (CSV). Everything you pick is in the address.
- Each row: the change, the record, who and their role, an Undo state badge, the time (Indian time) and one button: Undo, Unban, Restore vN (page publishes), or Redo for a change that was undone. Locked rows show the lock reason ("Money sent") and "Never". Rows past their window say "Time passed"; a change this person's role cannot undo says "Not for your role"; kinds whose undo is not built yet say "Not available yet".
- A button runs `undoAction` (the A3 core), which restores only from the data saved on the audit row. After Undo a toast offers Undo again (that is Redo); Redo on a row runs the undo of the undo.
- A row opens a drawer: what changed field by field (before and after from the audit row, secrets stripped), the reason, the record's earlier changes, and the button.
- "What can be undone" card shows the real windows (read from the site settings) and the things that can never be undone.
- `src/server/admin/history.ts`: `listHistory`, `historyDetail`, `historyCsv`, and `recordHistory(ctx, {targetType, targetId})`, the per-record history function the drawers can use.
- `/admin/history/export/` returns the filtered list as CSV (up to 5,000 rows); a cell that starts like a formula is quoted with a leading apostrophe.

## Not done
- The Versions / Decisions / Money tabs of the ad history drawer (mockup 03b) are the ad edit card's job; `recordHistory` gives it the audit part.
- "Who" lists up to 40 people who changed something in the filtered period.
