# T9.3

SEO and growth on the same branch as the light layout work.

- Sitemap index at `/sitemap.xml` with shards for categories, cities, districts, tags, ads, and content. Each shard has lastmod. `robots.txt` links the index even when `SITE_NOINDEX` is set. Tag URLs are included only when an active ad uses that tag.
- Public pages use `revalidate` (300s listings and hubs, 60s ads). The root layout calls `connection()` only when `MONGODB_URI` is missing, so a build with the database prerenders ISR and a build without it stays dynamic and still compiles. Publishing an ad calls `revalidatePath`.
- Images go through `next/image` with AVIF and WebP. Fonts use `display: swap` and preload. Card and gallery boxes keep explicit aspect ratios.
- Ads expose Product + Offer (INR, InStock, seller display name) and BreadcrumbList. Home keeps WebSite + SearchAction + Organization. City, district, and category hubs add BreadcrumbList, ItemList, and FAQPage. Landing copy is admin-editable at `/admin/landings/` with a template fallback that changes with the city and category.
- Gurgaon is the Haryana city (old slug gurugram redirects). Districts: DLF, Sohna Road, Golf Course Road, MG Road, Cyber City, Sector 29. H1 example: Dating in Gurgaon.
- Tag pages use a self canonical and a title. They are indexable when at least one ad matches and noindex when empty. Staging `SITE_NOINDEX` still forces noindex. Bulk tags are at `/admin/tags/`.
- Canonicals are self-referencing, drop tracking params, and send hreflang `en-IN` and `x-default` at the same URL. Page 2 and empty photo or verified filters are noindex, follow, and stay linked.
- Promotions: Top still accepts 1, 3, or 7 days and can be scheduled up to 30 days ahead. Super Top, Highlight, and New show on cards. Bump refreshes `bumpedAt`. Orders can auto-renew from the wallet. Credit packs with bonus credits are tagged Extra credits. Coupons already apply. Free ads per week is `Site.freeAdsPerWeek` or `FREE_ADS_PER_WEEK`. ID, age, and photo badges come from admin KYC approval (or the seeded verified badge) and render on cards and ad pages.
- Saved ads write to the Favourite collection when a session exists and stay in local storage otherwise. Saved searches post to `/api/alerts/` and the expire cron emails matches through Brevo, or the console mailer when no key is set. Recently viewed stays on this device. `/manifest.webmanifest` plus the existing mark and favicon. No push.
- Open Graph and Twitter cards use absolute image URLs. The default image is `/brand/og-default.png`.
