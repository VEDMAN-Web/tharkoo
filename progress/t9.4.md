# T9.4 URL and slug manager

Admin screen `/admin/urls/` edits category, state, city, district, tag, page, blog, and ad slugs, plus the public prefixes stored on the site document. `src/lib/urls.ts` is the one builder used by links, the sitemap, canonicals, and JSON-LD. Defaults match the existing routes.

A slug or prefix change writes a 301. Chains collapse, loops are rejected, and `src/proxy.ts` applies the cached table. The same screen edits redirects (including hit counts and CSV) and per-URL title, description, H1, canonical, noindex, and OG image. Saves revalidate the layout and sitemap shards.

Checked with `tests/server/urls/urls.test.ts`: a city rename, a category rename, and an ad-prefix change keep the old URLs redirecting, and the sitemap uses the new URLs.
