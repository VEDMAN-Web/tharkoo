# T9.2 progress

Homepage and chrome are editable from the admin. Post-ad, the search control, portraits, and the staging audit are on the same branch.

`graphify` is not installed (`NO_GRAPHIFY`). No `graphify-out/` was created.

## Homepage editor

- Storage is `Site.homepage.content`. Defaults match the current page until an admin saves.
- `/admin/homepage/` edits the hero, search placeholder, post label, category cards, city groups, SEO, age gate, and cookie copy.
- `/admin/menus/` edits the header and footer columns.
- Save, reorder, show/hide, and Reset to default. Reset replaces the whole document after a confirm.
- Only admin and superadmin. Text is sanitised. Audit actions are `site.homepage.update` and `site.homepage.reset`.
- Public pages read through the `site` cache tag. Save invalidates that tag and revalidates the layout.
- The post button label is editable. Its destination stays `/u/post-insert/`.

## Post ad

A signed-out visit to `/u/post-insert/` redirects to `/u/login/?next=/u/post-insert/` before the wizard renders. Register on staging signs the person in and lands on the wizard. Login does the same. `next` rejects open redirects.

Step 2 uploads a photo. Cloudinary is used when its env vars are set. Otherwise the file is stored on disk and served from `/uploads/[file]`. JPEG, PNG, and WebP up to 8 MB. ID photos stay private.

Publishing still requires manual admin approval. The wizard shows “Your ID needs a quick check before this ad can go live.” and a Verify ID link to `/u/account/kyc/`. After an admin approves the check, submit sets the ad active. It then appears in My Ads and on the public ad URL.

## Audit

Search button is a 34×34 circle. The city text is the only flex-growing span.

Mobile gallery shows the first photo large with SHOW ALL. Desktop gallery is a 2×2 cover grid.

Super Top and other cards use a fixed 4:5 photo slot with cover and no overlap.

Every city and category has 12–20 ads (16 in the top cities, 12 elsewhere). Delhi districts central-delhi, new-delhi, and south-delhi resolve on the city, including when a state shares the slug.

Share links are absolute. The ad JSON-LD is a Product with an INR offer, seller, and image, plus BreadcrumbList. `/sitemap.xml` lists cities, districts, blog, help, CMS, legal, and every active ad, each with lastmod. The catalogue is under 50,000 URLs, so it stays one sitemap. `SITE_NOINDEX=true` forces `noindex, nofollow`.

`/about/` and `/grievance/` redirect to the CMS pages. Empty login and register do not call the API. A 10-digit phone starting 6–9 is accepted as +91.
