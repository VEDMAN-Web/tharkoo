# H19.8: Video call switch and price inside each category (admin)

## Done
1. Category edit form (Details tab) and the New category dialog have a "Video calls" section: switch "Video calls: On/Off", "Price per call (credits)" and the line "Users pay this per call. The vendor keeps 70 %, Tharkoo keeps 30 %." The percents come from the H19.10 money setting (Tharkoo share = N, vendor = 100 - N), read on the server and passed to the form; nothing is hard-coded.
2. Same server rules as the Video tours page: the form saves through `updateVideoTourSettings` (superadmin only, audit row "video_tours.settings.update", the people rule and the kind rule). A locked switch is shown off and disabled with the reason under it (people listing, kind without video, no kind yet). A non-superadmin sees the section read only ("Only a superadmin changes video calls"). In the New dialog the section follows the chosen kind; after the category is created the switch and price are saved with the same function.
3. One source of truth: the category form, the Video tours page and the server all use the category's own `videoTourEnabled` and `videoTourCoins`; the rules (floor 10 = TOUR_MIN_PRICE_COINS, max 100, lock reasons, the price check) are in one module, `src/app/admin/settings/video-tours/shared.ts`, used by both screens. A test saves through the shared rules and reads the same value from the form data and from the Video tours list.

## Notes
- The price floor is enforced on the server (`updateVideoTourSettings`, from H19.10) and in the form (10 to 100).
- No new money logic; no other file outside the card's globs was changed.
