- `src/server/layout/d1-home.ts` (new) and `scripts/migrate/d1-home-layout.ts`: D1. Refuses a database that is not local unless `--allow-live`. Refuses to replace a format 2 home that was edited; does nothing when already identical. Never turns the switch on.
