# H16.8 (FIX-18): h7-104-home "photos swipe by themselves ... and pause after a click"

Cause: the test, not the product. The test hovers a Home card and clicks the card's own "Next photo" arrow. After 350 ms the hover pop-out opens and covers that arrow (it has its own arrow, which a visitor clicks). Clicking the covered arrow made the browser scroll the page by about 480 px to find a spot to click, which left the card less than half on screen (measured: card top at 773 px on a 900 px screen). A card that is less than half on screen does not swipe by design (VISIBLE_SHARE 0.5), so the last step, "it swipes again after the pause", could never happen. The pause logic itself is right: after a manual step the card is calm for 6 s and then moves on, as long as it is on screen.

Fix: the test waits for the pop-out and clicks the pop-out's "Next photo" arrow, like a visitor does. That arrow goes through the same calm and index code. No product change.

Verified: before, the last step failed (the slide stayed at 1 for 24 s in a probe); after, the test passes (1 passed at 1440 on a build of main).
