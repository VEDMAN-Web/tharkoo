# H10.1 People protection that a rename cannot break

## Done
- Leaving People: in Categories and fields, changing a category's Kind away from People asks first ("This turns off the adults-only check and the no-video rule for this category. Continue?"). The server enforces the same: without the yes it refuses. The change is its own audited write (category.kind) with an Undo; the Saved toast carries the Undo.
- The age check: every people category (People kind, a people address, or an age field) always has an age field on the post form and on the server, even when its own form has none (a renamed category with only an Interest field). The age is required, 18 to 99. The server adds the age field to the category's fields for posting, editing and import, and the publish gate refuses a people ad with no age (AGE_REQUIRED, "Enter your age (18 or over) to post in this category.") or under 18.
- Tests: vitest for the gate, the field rule, the confirm, the audit and the Undo, and a real submit of a renamed people ad; e2e on a renamed people category (the Kind question, Cancel, Continue, Undo; and the age field on a laptop and a phone).
