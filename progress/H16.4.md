# H16.4: ad page SEO (Opus #399)

Done
1. Title: `<Ad title> in <City> - <Category> | Tharkoo` (the ad title is cut at a word after 60 characters). Meta description: the first 150 or so characters of the ad's own text, cut at a word, with web links, e-mail addresses and phone numbers removed; an ad with almost no text keeps the generic description. (src/lib/seo/ad-seo.ts)
2. Jobs: the JobPosting JSON-LD already built only from fields the ad really has (title, description, date posted, validThrough = the ad's end date, hiring company, job location) and is left out when one is missing. Other categories keep the breadcrumb and add an Offer only where the ad has a price. A unit test checks the shape.
3. Ads sitemap: listed with lastmod since H16.5 (every live ad set to be indexed and every live sample ad); a test reads it.
4. IndexNow: an ad that goes live, changes or ends is queued for the search engines when INDEXNOW_KEY is set. Owner posts, expiry and owner moves already did; staff approve, edit, remove, bulk and Undo now do too, through the one hook staff changes already use (revalidateListingPages).
5. Ended ads: an expired or removed ad stays at its address with 200 for 30 days. The page says "This ad is no longer available", shows similar ads (same category and city, then the category), is noindex, and shows none of the ad's own text, photos or contact. After 30 days (counted from its expiry, or from the day it was removed) the address answers 410. An ad its owner deleted is 410 at once. Text keys ad.ended.title, ad.ended.text, ad.ended.more.
6. People-category ad pages add `noimageindex` to the robots meta (the text may rank, the photos stay out of Google Images).

Files: src/lib/seo/ad-seo.ts (new), src/server/listings/ended-ad.ts (new), src/components/AdEnded.tsx (new), src/server/listings/visibility.ts, revalidate.ts, src/lib/ui/meta.ts, text.ts, src/app/ad/[slug]/page.tsx, and the tests listed in docs/ownership/H16.4.json.

Verified: unit tests (title, description, robots, JobPosting shape, ended window, ended page content, noimageindex on a people ad, IndexNow queued) and the updated expiry tests (ad-lifetime, seo-delta: the old rule was 410 at the expiry instant). e2e at 1440 and 390: title of a live ad, an ended ad is 200 with the message and similar ads and without its own text, an ad ended 40 days ago is 410.

Notes: SITE_NOINDEX is untouched. Ad addresses are built with adPath; H16.2 changes them, and this change follows (the proxy and the page both use listingVisibility).
