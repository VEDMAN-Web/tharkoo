# H17.1: sample-ad honesty (Opus #412)

A sample (demo) ad is a showcase, not a person. It must not claim things only a real advertiser can.

## What changed
1. No trust claims on a sample ad. In `presentRows` (src/server/search/query.ts) a sample ad has `verified = false` and no trust marks, even when its owner account is approved or the ad is marked verified. So no "Photo verified" tag on a card, a row, a pop-out or the ad page, and no verified pill or seller line on the ad page.
2. No online state and no contact buttons on a sample ad. Cards and rows and the hover pop-out never show the online dot or a Chat button (`ad.sample`); the contact provider does not ask for a presence (no Online now or Away). On the ad page (new and legacy design) the Call, WhatsApp and Chat buttons and the phone bottom bar are not drawn at all; the Sample note and its Post an ad button stay (the pill too).
3. The words and meta of a listing page count REAL ads only. `queryListings` now also gives `realTotal` (the same list without sample ads), carried through the feeds. The H1 text, the meta description, the intro, the questions and the SEO variable `{count}` of the city page, the district page, the category page and the sub-category page use it. With no real ad they say a new UI text (`listing.sampleCount`, "Sample ads shown until local ads are posted", editable in Menus & site text under Listing pages) instead of "0 live ads". The list, the pager and the filter "Show N" are unchanged: they show what is on the page, sample ads included.
4. No views and no clicks on a public card. The H14.1 row cards (list pages, search, similar ads, the hover pop-out) no longer show "N views, N clicks" or fetch the counters; the counts stay in the seller dashboard. Home cards never showed them.

## Tests (written first)
- tests/server/listings/h17-1-sample-honesty.test.ts (5): a sample ad has no verified flag and no trust marks while a real ad keeps both; `realTotal` counts real ads only (0 with only samples); the landing words say the sample line with no real ads and the number with real ads; the key has these words.
- tests/e2e/h17-1-sample-honesty.e2e.ts (1440 and 390): a sample ad page has no verified text, no verified pill, no online dot, no bottom bar, no Call, WhatsApp or Chat button or tel/WhatsApp link, and keeps the Sample banner with its Post an ad link; a listing page with sample ads only has the sample line in its meta and no "N live ads" in its words, and its cards have no views, clicks, verified tag or online dot; a listing page with a real ad says "<real count> live ads" and not the count with samples.
- Updated on purpose (the old checks asserted what this card removes):
  - h14-1-listing-rows: the card no longer has the stats (checks they are absent); the "views and clicks read with their word" test now checks the card shows none, whatever the counters say, and the footer fits.
  - h14-2c-row-cards: the "stats (empty space)" click target is gone with the stats.
  - h7-91-filter-column: "a list card shows its real views and clicks" is now "shows no views, no clicks and no owner counts".
  - ad-page-content: the similar-ads row cards have no stats.
  - h16-5-showcase-demo: a sample ad used to answer Chat and WhatsApp with the sample message; it now has no such buttons (the banner and Post button are still checked).
  - home-ad-cards (failing on main since H16.5): the pills test allows the Sample ad pill on DEMO cards only, requires it there, forbids it on real cards, and still requires category and city only otherwise (Opus asked not to delete the check).

## Tests that opened a sample ad and expected contact to work (red on main since H16.5, now using a real ad)
ad-page.e2e.ts (Chat opens at once), contact-card.e2e.ts (the people ad buttons, the property ad buttons, the video call sign-in, the no-coins tour, the tablet bar), visitor.e2e.ts (ad page: gallery, contact reveal, share, report), ad-page-content.e2e.ts (right card, sticky card, bottom bar, picture height) and new-live-pages.e2e.ts (gallery, details, contact panel). They use the new helper `realAdPath` in tests/e2e/helpers.ts: a copy of the feed's first ad owned by a throwaway real account, with a photo and an Online now seller, kept out of every list (`feedHidden`) so no other spec sees it. Each check keeps its meaning (the buttons work on a real ad); the new h17-1 spec asserts that on a sample ad there are no such buttons, only the note and its Post button.
