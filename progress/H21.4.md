# H21.4 — post-ad redesign: Step 4, About you

## Done
- Step 4 (`AboutYouStep.tsx`, `about-step.css`), in the design's order: the note "We remember these, so next time this step is already filled in.", **Name on your ads** (counter N / 20, "A first name or nickname is fine."), the **age** for people ads, **Phone number** with +91 in front of the box (`inputmode=tel`, hint "Shown on your ad so people can call you."), then "Also reach me on": a WhatsApp switch (with "Same number as my phone", or its own number box when unticked) and a Telegram switch (an @ box, required when on). Switches are `role=switch`, 52 x 30 px. Step title "About you", lead "How people will see you and reach you."
- No city field here: the city is chosen in step 2. A first-time account still saves its name (and the city picked in step 2) to its profile when it moves on; a date of birth that is missing still sends the person to their profile as before.
- The name and the age moved out of Details: step 2 no longer shows or asks for them. `stepOfError` now puts `nickname` and `age` in step 4, so Continue on step 2 never stops for them and step 4 does. The review row for the name edits step 4.
- Fills from the last ad: the first time step 4 opens with nothing filled in, the phone, WhatsApp, Telegram and name are read from the account's most recent other ad. Anything already typed stays.
- Preview on this step is the details card with the "Posted by" line outlined and tagged "Editing"; on a phone the Preview button opens the same card.
- Also (small fix from Opus): while a photo is dragged, the slot it left is the design slot, pale pink #fff0f7 with a 2 px dashed #c2126f border (it was dark purple because an older rule won). The photos e2e now checks those colours during a drag and saves a screenshot of the drag state.
- New text keys `post.v2.about.*` only. STATUS.md: H21.3 approved, H21.4 row added.
