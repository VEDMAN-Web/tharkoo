# H17.9: one posting-price message site-wide

The message is "Your first 3 ads are free." (Vedant's decision, Opus #418). It is defined once (`FIRST_ADS_FREE` in src/lib/ui/text.ts) and every default text that speaks about the price of posting uses it. Each one stays a Site text key, so the admin can change it on Site text. No price and no credit logic is touched.

Every change (before -> after)
1. `home.banner.sub` (Home hero line): "Free ads across India. Chat with advertisers online and verify on a video call." -> "Your first 3 ads are free. Chat with advertisers online and verify on a video call."
2. `demo.message` (sample ad note when someone taps Chat or Call): "... how ads look on Tharkoo. Post yours free" -> "... how ads look on Tharkoo. Your first 3 ads are free."
3. `myads.cost.free` (My ads, cost line when posting is free for everyone): "Posting an ad is free." -> "Your first 3 ads are free."
4. `price.allFreeText` (price card on the credits page when paid posting is off): "Posting an ad is free for now." -> "Your first 3 ads are free."
5. `howitworks.cost.free` (How it works, "What does it cost to post?" when paid posting is off): "Posting an ad is free. Chatting and calling the advertiser are free. ..." -> "Your first 3 ads are free. Chatting and calling the advertiser are free. ..."
6. How it works / Terms copy (src/content/trust.ts, the posting steps): "A free weekly limit applies." -> "Your first 3 ads are free; after that the price is shown before you publish."
7. AI draft prompt (src/server/content/studio/ai-draft.ts, never shown to visitors): "a free classifieds website" -> "a classifieds website".

Checked and left as they are (they already carry the condition, or are about something else)
- `howitworks.cost.freeThenFee` ("Your first {free} ads are free. After that each ad costs ..., shown before you publish"), `howitworks.cost.fee` (fee from the first ad, used only when the account has no free ads), `price.firstFree`, `myads.cost.freeLeft*`, `post.pay.free`, the post-ad pay card and the price card rows: they read the live setting.
- "Chat for free", "Chat with advertisers stays free", `home.utility.ticker1` ("Chat with advertisers for free"), `ad.call.chatFirst`: they are about chat, not posting.
- /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt: they say nothing about the price of posting, so nothing to change (no new claim added).
- Footer, help list ("Free contact limits can apply" is about contact), meta descriptions and page titles in code: none says "free to post" or "free classifieds". Titles and descriptions the admin has typed live in the database and are not in code; none could be read from here.
- The "FREE" badge, "free ads left" dots and the Free promotion plan are the real free-ad counters and a promotion plan, not a posting promise.
