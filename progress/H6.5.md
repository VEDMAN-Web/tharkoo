# H6.5: Live video tours (paid in coins)

REVIEW PLEASE: this PR touches escrow, the ledger, a provider webhook and the proxy exemption. Opus line-review requested in relay #38 point 4.

## Done (server and API)
- Category fields: `kind` (goods, property, vehicle, job, service, people; a missing kind fails closed), `videoTourEnabled` (default off), `videoTourCoins` (default 1, 1 to 100). Subcategory `videoTourEnabled` overrides the category when set (null inherits). Listing `videoVerifiedUntil`.
- People rule in the database, not in a registry: `holdInEscrow` now requires `listingId` and calls `assertVideoTourAllowed` (`src/server/wallet/listing-guard.ts`). It refuses a missing ad, a missing or unknown kind, a people category, or an off switch. The registry stays as an optional extra guard.
- Flow (`src/server/tours/service.ts`):
  - `requestTour`: buyer pays the category price into escrow; the advertiser gets an email. One open tour per buyer and ad (partial unique index). Own ad refused. Advertisers with 3 reversed disputes are refused.
  - `acceptTour` (30-minute window; a late accept refunds), `declineTour` (refund).
  - `endTour` (provider webhook): a call of 120 s or more pays the advertiser and sets the 90-day badge; anything shorter, or a failed call, refunds. The first event wins; replays change nothing.
  - `expireDueTours` (called from the existing cron): refunds requests not accepted within 30 minutes; accepted tours with no call-end after 4 hours are refunded.
  - `disputeTour`: buyer, within 48 hours of the call ending.
  - `reverseTour`: staff with trust_safety, finance, admin or superadmin move the coin from vendor earnings back to the wallet. If the coins were already withdrawn, it stops and asks for a manual fix.
  - `updateVideoTourSettings`: superadmin only. A people category can never be switched on; changing a category to people turns its switch off.
- Routes: `src/app/api/tours/[[...slug]]/route.ts` (list mine, request, accept, decline, dispute, reverse, GET/PUT admin categories and settings) and `src/app/api/tours/webhook/route.ts` (signature checked with the shared verifier; refusals audited by reason only).
- Proxy: `/api/tours/webhook` added to the exact provider-path set (the only Origin exemption).
- Room provider: `src/server/tours/room.ts`. Mock by default; `daily` throws until `DAILY_API_KEY` exists.

## Not done
- No UI: the ad-page "Request live video tour · 1 coin" button, the advertiser's accept/decline view, the dashboard notification, the Settings → Video tours screen, the "Video verified" badge on ads, and the report-a-call form. The API is ready for each of them.
- The mock room has no join page (`joinUrl` is null).
- Admin Finance does not list disputed or reversed tours yet (the reversal is API-only).
- The people guard and the switch are server-side only; the greyed-out switch in the UI is part of the missing screen.

## Assumptions to review
- An accepted tour with no call-end event is refunded after 4 hours (the card did not give a number).
- A reversed dispute stops only that advertiser's future tours after 3 reversals (counted from reversed tours).
- Dispute reasons are plain text, up to 500 characters.
