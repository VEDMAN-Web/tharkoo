- Step 1 eyebrow "Step 1 of 5" shows on a computer; lead line is "Pick a category. It helps the right people find your ad." The price is no longer in the lead line (the note says it).
- Rail label for step 4 is "About you" (`post.rail.contact`).
- Text keys `post.v2.cat.*` only; two default texts changed: `post.stepCopy.basicsHelp` and `post.rail.contact`.
- STATUS.md: H21.0 and H21.8 approved, H21.1 row added.
