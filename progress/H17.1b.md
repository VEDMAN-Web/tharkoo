# H17.1b: the H7.4d chat panel test uses a real ad (follow-up of H17.1)

tests/e2e/h7-4d-followups.e2e.ts "the chat panel closes on an outside click and on Escape" opened the first ad of the Property feed, which is a sample ad. Since H16.5 a sample ad answers Chat with the sample note (no panel), so it was red on main; with H17.1 a sample ad has no Chat button at all, and the test's own `test.skip(chat count is 0)` would have turned it into a silent skip.
Now it opens a REAL ad (the `realAdPath` helper added in H17.1), and the silent skip is gone: the chat button must be visible, then the panel opens, closes on an outside click on a computer, and closes on Escape. The check keeps its meaning and nothing is weakened. Verified on a build of H17.1: all the H7.4d specs pass (the chat panel test 4.0 s at 390 and 3.7 s at 1440, not skipped). The unused local helper `anyAdPath` and the unused `Page` import were removed.
This PR is stacked on H17.1 (#449) because it uses its helper.
