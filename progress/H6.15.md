# H6.15: Admin create ad, bulk import link, category photo limit

Lane E, relay #58. Branch `haiku/h6.15-create-ad`.

## Done

- **Create ad screen** at `/admin/ads/create/` (admin and superadmin). Sections: who the ad is for, location, category and details, photos, videos, publish.
- **On behalf of an account**: the staff account, or any existing account by email. Uses the import's owner checks (account active, verified). Audited with the staff actor and the owner (`listing.admin_create`, `after.ownerId`).
- **Location**: country, state, city, district from Locations. Any country works. A missing city is added with "Add new city" under the chosen state, through the existing `createPlace` (slug checked, audited `location.create`). A country with no states cannot take a new city yet (see Not done).
- **Category, sub-category and fields**: the category's own field rules (`parseListingAttributes`, same as post-ad and import), sub-category required when the category has any. Post-ad text rules: title at least 5 letters or numbers, description at least 20 characters, banned-word check, publish gate.
- **Photos**: many at once, thumbnails, remove, make cover, move up and down. Limit = category `importMaxPhotos`, default 10. Uploads go one at a time to `/admin/ads/photos/` (staff only, audited `media.admin_upload`).
- **Videos**: up to 2, mp4/webm/mov, 10 MB and 30 seconds (client and server checks).
- **Publish**: superadmin can publish now; otherwise moderation. Lifetime uses the existing `adLifetimeMs` through `finalise`.
- **Sidebar**: "Create ad" and "Bulk import" (`/admin/import/`) under the Listings group in `src/components/admin/nav.ts`. The brief said "Ads"; no Ads group exists, so the links sit in Listings.
- **Categories**: "Max photos for admin posts and imports (1 to 30)" control in Admin > Categories. Server validation `cleanImportMaxPhotos` (blank = default 10). Audited in `category.update`.
