# H15.7 (FIX-7, E1-B6): saved ads of a signed-in visitor come from the account

## Cause
Saved ads were read only from the browser's own storage, so a signed-in visitor saw different lists on different devices, and the Saved ads page had no way to take an ad off the list.

## Fix
- `src/app/api/saved/route.ts`: the account's list now also returns what a saved card needs (photo, place, time), newest first.
- `src/components/ui/SaveButton.tsx`: `loadAccountSaved()` reads the account's list; ads saved on the device while signed out go up to the account once; an ad saved while signed in (`acct`) that is missing from the account was removed on another device and is dropped, so a removed ad does not come back. The device copy mirrors the account so every heart agrees. `removeSaved()` takes an ad off the device and, when signed in, off the account. Hearts compare by address as well as id.
- `src/components/buyer/SavedPanel.tsx` (Saved ads page) and `SavedAdsRail.tsx` (account home): signed-in visitors see the account's list ("N saved in your account"); a Remove button on each saved ad on the page. A visitor who is not signed in keeps device saving ("N saved on this device") and Remove works there too.

## Test (first: failing on main, then passing)
`tests/e2e/h15-7-saved-account.e2e.ts` (two separate browsers stand for two devices):
1. an ad saved on device 1 shows on device 2 after signing in, Remove on device 2 clears it, and device 1 no longer has it;
2. a visitor who is not signed in keeps saving on the device and can Remove;
3. ads saved on the device before signing in go up to the account, so another device sees them.
- On main: 4 failed (tests 1 and 2, both projects).
- With the fix: all 3 pass at 1440 and 390 (12 of 12 in two runs in a row). With user-panel, e1a journeys and h14-2b: 54 passed.
