# H17.2: sitemaps and caching

Done
1. Sitemap `lastmod` is the record's real date, and is left out when there is none (src/lib/seo/sitemaps.ts, the fallback in src/app/sitemap/[shard]/route.ts). Ads: the ad's updatedAt (else its publish date). Category hub: the category's updatedAt. State page: the newest of the state's and the category's. Category in a city and a city's areas: the newest of the city (or area), the category and the newest live ad there. City hub (/cities/x/): the city's updatedAt. Blog, help and CMS pages: their updatedAt. The fixed pages (home, terms, privacy, cookies, contact and the like) have no record that keeps a date, so they have no lastmod (before, they all said "now" on every request).
2. The 404 page has ONE robots meta. Next adds its own `noindex` to a not-found page, and the page also set its own, so there were two. The page's own is removed.
3. Caching:
   - Ad page, location page and district page now revalidate every 300 seconds (the category page already did). The existing on-demand revalidation (revalidateListingPages, the same paths) is not touched, so a change still shows at once.
   - The shared in-process cache (src/lib/cache.ts) now keeps an entry that is past its time for up to ten times its time (at most 10 minutes) and refreshes it once in the background, so the first visitor after a quiet minute no longer waits for a cold load. A change that clears the tags removes the entry, so a change is never answered from an old copy. The cache also holds 120 entries instead of 20: each category and city page has its own feed key, and the old limit pushed one page's data out for the next.
   - Honest note on ISR: a page is stored by Next as a whole only when its build had the database (the e2e build has none, by design) and only when the page does not read the query string. The ad page qualifies; the category and city pages read `searchParams` (filters, page number), so Next renders them for every visit and their speed comes from the data cache above. A page-level cache for the plain city and category addresses would need an internal rewrite to a copy of the page that does not read the query string; that is a bigger change, not done here.

Measured (production build of main vs this branch, the e2e server with the database grown to about 1,750 places like the live one after the cities import: +834 cities, +801 areas; each time is time to first byte in ms, one local machine, no network latency to the database):

| page | cold (first hit) before | cold after | first hit after 70 s idle, before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a city with no ads (new) | 557 | 372 | 997 | 334 |
| a second new city | 117 | 103 | 460 | 106 |
| category hub | 146 | 111 | 276 | 92 |
| a city with ads | 99 | 96 | 157 | 97 |
| an ad page | 281 | 106 | 169 | 216 (noise) |

Warm hits are about 65 to 120 ms before and after. Every visit, warm or cold, still makes about 20 database calls (31 on a cold page, 47 on an ad page): with a database that is 80 ms away that is what makes a cold city page slow on the live site (4.0 s seen), and the stale window removes the cold repeats after every quiet minute but not the first visit after a deploy. Cutting the calls per page (a follow-up) is the next step.

Files: src/lib/cache.ts, src/lib/seo/sitemaps.ts, src/app/sitemap/[shard]/route.ts, src/app/not-found.tsx, the three page files (one line each), and the tests in docs/ownership/H17.2.json.

Verified: unit tests for the dates (real, left out when none, never today for a fixed page), for the cache (fresh, stale answered at once and refreshed once, a change never answered from the old copy, beyond the window it waits, a failed refresh keeps the held copy), and for the 300 s revalidate values; e2e: a missing page has exactly one robots meta, the content sitemap has no made-up dates, the ads sitemap has real dates.
