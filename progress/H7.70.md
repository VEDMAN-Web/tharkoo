# H7.70: no cut faces in banners and photos

## Done
- Focus points: every image row in Menus and footer (Photos and listing bands) has a focus point for the card photo, the desktop band and the phone band (click picker), and the login image has one in the homepage admin. The slots use them as object-position or background-position. Card photos default to 50% 18%; the desktop band to 72% 12%; the phone band to 50% 12%; the login image to 50% 20%. The default band (home hero) uses the hero's own focus points.
- Band photos are read independently: the desktop band uses "Band desktop"; under 768 px the phone band uses "Band phone" when set, else "Band desktop" (and the other way round when only a phone photo exists).
- Card aspect ratios are unchanged (4:3 city and category cards, 4:5 on a phone, ad cards 4:5, blog covers stay a gradient until covers exist).
- City page: the category photo cards below the list are hidden from 820 px (the left column has the same links) and stay on phones.
- Phone band: under 600 px only the last two crumbs show; the full trail stays in the page's JSON-LD.
- Contact sheet and slot inventory in .relay/shots/1-11/.
