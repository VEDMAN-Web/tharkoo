# H6.7: Ads live 72 hours

## Done
- Admin setting `adLifetimeHours` on the site settings (default 72, clamped 1 to 720). Read by `adLifetimeMs()` for new ads, renewals, approvals (moderation) and CSV import (`src/server/listings/lifetime.ts`).
- Expiry is exact: an ad is live until its expiry instant and gone (410) at it. The 30-day "ended" window is gone; `listingVisibility` returns `gone` on expiry.
- Ad pages are `noindex` (follow by default) while live. Ad pages are removed from the sitemap (`shardEntries("ads")` is empty).
- Paid boosts never run past the ad's expiry. Choice: the simpler option. A boost that would end after expiry is refused at purchase; an auto-renewal that would end after expiry is switched off. No partial charges (`src/server/billing/boost-window.ts`).
- Demo ads expire from the seed time. `refreshDemoAds` renews only the seed user's ads, superadmin only, only when `SITE_NOINDEX=true`, audited.
- Tests: `tests/server/listings/ad-lifetime.test.ts` (setting clamp, expiry boundary, boost boundary, renew path, sitemap, refresh guards). Updated `tests/server/ui/seo-delta.test.ts`, `tests/server/urls/urls.test.ts`, `tests/server/search/hidden-ads.test.ts` to the new rules (they expected 30-day "ended" pages and ad sitemap entries).

## Not done
