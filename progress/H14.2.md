# H14.2: the whole ad card is the link (part 1: Home, chat, post success, promote)

Vedant's rule: anyone clicking anywhere on an ad card opens that ad; no "View ad" / "View my ads" buttons.
Pattern: one real link on the title, stretched over the card (`::after`, inset 0); every other control sits above it; no nested links; pointer cursor; a focus ring on the card when the link has keyboard focus.

## Part 1 (this PR)
- Home card (`HomeAdCard`, `home-ads.css`): it already used the stretched title link; added the pointer cursor and the card focus ring (`:has(.tkh-ad-link:focus-visible)`, the link's own outline is off). No layout change (only `cursor` and `outline`).
- Hover pop-out (`HomeAdPopout`): the View ad button is gone; the pop-out title is the stretched link (one click event per open, it was not counted before); heart, arrows, thumbnails, pills and Chat sit above it; Chat stays.
- Chat Inbox thread header: "View ad" replaced by a small ad card (photo, title, price), the new shared `MiniAdCard` (the whole card opens the ad). The conversation list now carries `adPrice` (`src/server/chat/service.ts`).
- Post success: the "View your ad" link and the "View my ads" button are gone; the posted-ad card is the link; "Post another ad" stays (now the main button). `newSuccessCopy` no longer returns a `primary` button.
- Promote sheet: the "See my ads" link is gone (the sheet only knows the ad's title, not its address).
- Old text keys (`home.ads.viewAd`, `post.success.viewAd`, `post.stepCopy.viewMyAds`, `promote.viewAds`) stay in the stored settings and the defaults; they are just not used here. `labels.viewAd` stays in `HomeAdLabels` because ListingCards, NewAdDetail and not-found (H14.1's files) still pass it.

## Not in this PR (as planned)
- ListingCards, SimilarAds, NotFoundView, FreshAds: after H14.1 merges.
- User panel: MyAdsList, MyAdsBoard, SavedAdsRail, Saved ads page, Analytics rows: next PR.

## Acceptance (this part)
A1 photo, title and empty space open the ad (Home card, pop-out); A2 the heart does not; A3 no "View ad" / "View my ads" / "View your ad" / "See my ads" left in these places (checked in the specs); A4 Tab then Enter opens the ad with a visible ring; A5 one click event per open (counted inside the page); A6 no layout change on Home (only cursor and outline added; see the PR description for the pixel check).
