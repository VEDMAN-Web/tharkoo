# H16.6b: the empty band under the seller dashboard (Opus #408, follow-up of H16.6)

CSS only. Under the last card (Buy credits) the space to the footer was 152 px at 1440 (136 at 768, 104 at 390) while the gap between sections is 24 (16 on a phone). The extra was the page padding of the account pages (64 px, 48 px on small screens) on top of the footer's own top margin (88 px, 56 px on a phone).

Now, only on the seller dashboard page, the page padding under the last card is one section gap (24 px, 16 px on a phone), so the space to the footer is one section gap plus the footer's own margin: 112 px at 1440 and 768, 72 px at 390. The other account pages are unchanged.

File: src/components/buyer/dashboard/dashboard.css (`.up-page:has(.up-main > .up-dash)`).

Test: tests/e2e/h16-6b-dashboard-bottom.e2e.ts measures at 1440, 768 and 390: the gap between the last two sections (24 or 16), the space from the last card to the footer, and the footer's top margin; the space to the footer must equal the section gap plus the footer margin (within 1 px). On the old build it failed (152 against 112 at 1440). With the fix it passes, and h16-6-dashboard-align and h14-3-seller-dashboard (the dashboard specs) still pass: 5 passed, 3 skipped by design.
