# H8.18 (A18) Ad decisions: reject and remove with the credits choice, with undo

Done
- decide.ts: rejectListing({refund}) (default true so older callers keep their behaviour; the screen always sends the checkbox) and removeLiveAd({reason, refund}) for a live ad (status removed, public pages refreshed). Both go through adminWrite: permission ads.decide, reason 1 to 500, audit row (listing.reject / listing.remove) with before/after, undo window 7 days (Site.undoWindows.decisions).
- Money rule: the fee is given back only when the ad's fee is paid (isPostingFeePaid) AND refund is true; a free ad or an unticked box moves no credits. The refund uses the existing once:true ledger key per ad (no double refund).
- Undo (handlers/listing-decision.ts): sets the old status directly (never publishes, never charges a new fee). If credits were given back they are taken back as a new fee line, once, only when the seller still has them; otherwise the ad is restored and the undo line says "credits already spent, not taken back". Redo is offered only when no money moved.
- Screen: Held tab Reject..., Live tab Remove ad... (row and drawer), ReasonDialog with the reason list, an optional note and, for a paid ad only, "Give back the N credits to {seller}" (off by default). After a decision an Undo toast.
- Rows and the drawer carry paidCredits (what the ad's fee stands at now).

Decision for Opus (conflict in the docs)
- BUILD-PLAN A18 says undo after a refund "keeps the refund and does not debit"; UI-RULES owner decision (17 Nov) says undo takes the credits back while unspent and otherwise restores the ad and says so. I built the UI-RULES version, because keeping the refund and restoring the ad would give the seller the ad for free. The e2e checks the balance is back to what it was before the decision.
