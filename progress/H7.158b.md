# H7.158 part 2: end times in All ads and on the ad page

## Done
- All ads: an "Ends" column (India date and time, "in 5 h" / "in 12 min" / "in 3 days" / "ended"), a "Sort by" choice (Newest first, Ends soonest, Ends last) and the filter "Ends within 24 h" (live or paused ads ending in the next 24 hours). All in the address (`sort`, `ends=24`).
- Bulk "Extend..." (was "Extend 30 days") asks for +24 hours, +48 hours, +7 days or +30 days; new bulk "Set end..." picks a date and time (India time, future only, within a year). Both are one audited write per ad with one batch id and one Undo for the batch; a paused ad stays paused; ads that are not live or paused are skipped and listed. No credits move.
- Ad page, Status card: "Ends" with the time left, "Ends on (India time)" with "Set end time" (future only), quick buttons "+24 h", "+48 h", "Expire in 24 h" (end = now + 24 h) and "End now..." (a typed reason is required; the ad is expired at once, hidden from search at once). Each has an Undo toast. "Extend 30 days" is gone from this card (the bulk Extend... has it).
- Server: `bulkChangeAds` takes `hours` or `endsAt` and has the actions `setEnd`, `expireIn` and `endNow` (`listing.set_end`, `listing.end_now`); the existing `listing.decision` Undo handler puts the old status and end time back.

## Not done
- The old "extend" with no length still means 30 days for any caller that does not pass `hours`.
- "End now" is for one ad from the ad page; the list has no bulk End now.
