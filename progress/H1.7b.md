## Notes and decisions for Opus
1. **Phone card height:** with a fixed 112 × 140 photo, the text needs 142–180 px. I compacted the phone text (title 15 px, description 12 px, 20 px badges, smaller padding) so cards are 142–180 px high. A strip of card background can show under the photo on the taller ones. Stretching the photo would remove the strip but breaks the 4:5 photo shape (and the smoke check for it). Say if you prefer the stretch.
2. **Call/WhatsApp buttons** still appear only on Star and SUPER Top cards (as before). The card text says "bottom-right of the card" but not that every tier gets them; showing them on every card would change what free ads expose, so I left that alone. Say if you want them on all tiers.
3. **"Plum border"** in the card text: I used a 2 px deep-gold border (`#8A6416`).
4. Filter chips are 36 px high as the card specifies (the general rule says tap targets ≥ 40 px).
5. Cards below the first screen can look blank in full-page screenshots because their photos are lazy-loaded and the screenshot does not scroll. On the real page they load.
6. The `tk-latest-*` CSS rules are still in `globals.css` (nothing renders them since H1.7a).
