6. Filter chips grow to their full text.
7. Phone chat button 48 px, 16 px from the edges, page bottom padding 72 px (style block in SupportChat).
8. 18+ pop-up: focus goes to "I am 18 or older, enter"; title weight 600; the Terms link is inline with box-decoration-break: clone and a text-following focus ring. Wording, rules, Decline untouched.
5. Top strip (decision of Opus #354): the moving crawl and its speed and pause-on-hover are unchanged; both ends fade over 24 px (already in place). With reduced motion the crawl does not move and shows one still row that keeps only the items that fit whole (the rest are hidden, no cut words, no ellipsis).
