# H9.1 One way to buy credits

## Done
- One pack picker (src/components/recharge/CreditPacks.tsx) now sells credits on the Account home, /u/account/credits/, the Wallet Buy credits tab and the posting-flow recharge sheet. Same packs, same Pay button, same words.
- At most 4 packs first (the cheapest, the Most popular one, the best value, a bigger one; a pack that covers a shortfall always shows), with "See all packs" / "Show fewer packs" for the rest.
- Each pack shows total credits, the price in whole rupees (Rs 1,000, no .00), "+N free" only when the pack has bonus credits, and "About N ads" (credits divided by the posting fee from settings, rounded down; the line is hidden while paid posting is off or when it would read 0).
- One "Most popular" badge, pre-selected (the pack the admin tagged; with none, the cheapest starts selected). "Best value" is now worked out from the data (more credits per rupee than the cheapest pack) instead of the old admin text; "Extra credits" and "Best deal" are gone.
- The button is "Pay Rs X" and always has a pack. Under it: "Credits pay for posting and promoting ads." and, while test payments are on, "Test payments are on, so no real money is taken."
- /u/account/credits/ shows the packs inline (no button that opens a sheet). The Wallet sits in the account panel with the normal heading.
- Earnings tab and Withdraw show only when the account has earnings. The rule is one small helper: src/server/wallet/has-earnings.ts (userHasEarnings, earningsPresent). The panel summary carries it as summary.hasEarnings.
- The wallet balance card reads whole rupees.
