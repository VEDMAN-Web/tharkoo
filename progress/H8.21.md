Done
- /admin/reports/ rebuilt on the kit (it was a JSON editor over Page): tabs Open / Legal takedown / Closed with counts, search, ServerTable, drawer with the reports (reason, comment, age), seller and ad facts, a note box and the decision buttons: Dismiss (left), More (Pause ad, Remove ad and ban seller...), Remove ad... Each step has its own audit row and 7-day undo; the screen's Undo toast undoes all steps last first.
- report.decision undo handler built: restores each report's earlier status and dates and the ad's earlier status when the decision paused it; redo works.
- review.ts: paidCreditsFor is exported and connects first. a3-write.test.ts: report.decision counts as built.
