# H8.54: Menus and footer restyle

## Done
- /admin/menus/ (tab "Menus and footer") is a set of admin-kit cards: Header, About text and trust pills, Quick links, Top categories, Get in touch, Legal pills, Social links, Adults notice and copyright, and the short note about category and city pictures (kept, with its link to Categories & fields and Cities & areas).
- Link rows are one compact row each: a drag handle, Text, Link, Icon, Show, and three small quiet icon buttons (move up, move down, remove) with names for screen readers. The column names (Text, Link, Icon, Show) are written once above each list; on a phone the row is three short lines and the names are inside the fields. "Add a link" is a quiet button under each list. The Get in touch rows have Type, Label, Value and Link columns and a quiet remove.
- ONE sticky save bar (Unsaved changes, Discard, Save), a leave-without-saving guard (closing the tab and clicking any other admin link), and an Undo toast after Save. Save still goes through the same `saveShellConfig` (admin check, cleaning, unsafe-link check, audit row with before and after); Undo saves the version before the change through the same path, so it is audited too.
- The old read-only "Home page (New design)" block is gone (it still said "Ads live for 72 hours" and listed steps the Page builder owns). The a19 spec now checks it is gone.
- Social links: under the list, "On the site today" shows the six footer icons (WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Pinterest, Telegram) and where each goes now: a saved link, the default support address (WhatsApp, Telegram), or "no address yet: the icon shows greyed and opens nothing". While no link is saved here it reads the older social links of the site record, the way the footer does.
- Tap targets are at least 24 px for every control on the screen (checked in the e2e at 1440 and 390; the Show box is 24 px, the buttons 32 px, 44 px on a phone).
- No stored word was changed.
