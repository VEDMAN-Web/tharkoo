# H6.6b: Withdrawals, payouts queue, TDS/GST screen, wallet screens, phone OTP login

REVIEW PLEASE (ledger, payouts, auth). Do not merge without a human review.

## Done
- **Phone + OTP login.** `src/server/auth/otp.ts`: `OtpProvider` interface, mock (dev only; refuses to run in production), SMS stub that throws until `SMS_PROVIDER`/`SMS_API_KEY` are built. Sends limited to 3 per 10 minutes per phone number and per IP (Mongo counter via `consumeRateLimit`, key on sha256 of the number). A number not linked to an account gets the same answer with no SMS. Codes are HMAC-hashed, expire after 10 minutes, and are refused after 5 wrong guesses. A code is used up on success. Routes: `POST /api/auth/otp/send`, `POST /api/auth/otp/verify`. The login screen has the existing email form plus a "Log in with a code" section (`src/components/PhoneLogin.tsx`).
- **Phone linking.** `confirmPhone` now stores the confirmed number in `phone_identities` (one account per number). `startPhone` normalises the number. `src/components/PhoneLinkCard.tsx` on `/u/account/edit-account` lets a signed-in user link a number.
- **Payout KYC.** `src/server/wallet/payout-kyc.ts`: bank (legal name, PAN, account, IFSC) or UPI (legal name, PAN, UPI ID), zod-validated. Name, PAN, account, IFSC and UPI are encrypted with AES-256-GCM (`src/server/wallet/payout-crypto.ts`) using the key from `CONTACT_ENCRYPTION_KEY`. Only encrypted values are stored, and reads return masked values (PAN and account show the last 4). Submitted once; a second submit returns 409. Routes: `GET/PUT /api/wallet/payouts/kyc`.
- **Withdrawals.** `src/server/wallet/withdrawals.ts`. `POST /api/wallet/payouts/withdrawals {coins}` needs KYC on file, coins at or above the admin minimum (default 5), funds in `vendor_earnings`, and no other request inside the cooldown (default 3 days). The cooldown is an atomic counter (`withdraw-cooldown:<userId>`, max 1 per window). Coins move `vendor_earnings` to `payout_pending` through `postTransaction` (key `withdraw-hold:<id>`). Statuses: requested, approved, paid, failed, rejected.
  - Reject, and approved-then-failed (or a provider refusal), return the coins to `vendor_earnings` (key `withdraw-return:<id>`).
  - Paid moves `payout_pending` to `paid_out` (key `withdraw-paid:<id>`), with the bank or UPI reference recorded.
  - Each transition is a conditional status claim, so a double click cannot pay twice. If the money step fails, the status goes back.
  - Every admin write is audited (`payout.approve`, `payout.reject`, `payout.paid`, `payout.fail`).
- **PayoutProvider.** `src/server/wallet/payout-provider.ts`: interface, mock (`PAYOUT_PROVIDER` default; refused in production), and a bank stub that throws until `PAYOUT_API_KEY_ID`/`PAYOUT_API_KEY_SECRET` exist. A thrown error sends nothing and the payout stays requested. A returned refusal returns the coins.
- **Admin Finance.** `src/app/admin/finance/page.tsx` now has:
  - a Payouts queue (`PayoutsQueue.tsx`): table with CSV export, and approve, reject, mark paid (reference) and mark failed actions. Bank and UPI details are shown masked.
  - a TDS, GST and payout-rules form (`TaxSettingsForm.tsx`) on the existing `PUT /api/wallet/admin/settings`. No tax logic.
  - the existing refund-blocked table and pack editor are kept.
- **Wallet screens.** `/u/account/wallet/` (`src/components/wallet/CoinWallet.tsx`, `VendorEarnings.tsx`, `KycForm.tsx`, `format.ts`). Mobile first, gold theme, existing tokens. Tabs: Buy coins (packs from `/api/wallet/coins`), Earnings (available, pending, paid out, KYC, withdraw, withdrawal history), History (purchases with TDS and GST, coin movements).
- **Tests.** `tests/server/wallet/payouts.test.ts` (25) and `tests/server/auth/otp.test.ts` (13). They cover the minimum (default and admin-set), the cooldown and its reset, reject and fail returning coins, provider refusal and throw, paid, staff role checks, the route paths, KYC masking and encryption, one-time codes, wrong-guess lockout, expiry, the per-phone and per-IP send limit, the 429 route response, and the production mock refusal.

## Risks
- The cooldown counter is consumed before the ledger post. If the post fails after the counter is taken (rare; the balance is checked first), the vendor waits out one cooldown window with no request.
- Approve claims the status before calling the provider. If the process dies after the provider accepts but before `providerRef` is saved, the payout stays `approved` with no reference. It can be marked paid or failed by hand, and it cannot be approved (sent) twice.
- Ledger legs are two writes (ledger document, wallet entry for user_wallet) with no Mongo transactions. Same as H6.6a. Not touched here, since withdrawals only use vendor_earnings and payout_pending.
