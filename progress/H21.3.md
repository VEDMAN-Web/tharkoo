# H21.3 — post-ad redesign: Step 3, Photos

## Done
- Empty state (`PhotoStep.tsx`, `photos-step.css`): one dashed drop box ("Add photos of your work or place", hint line, Choose photos, Take a photo, Add video) and the note "Ads with 3 or more photos get far more replies. You can skip this and add photos later." The main button reads "Skip for now" while there is no photo and "Continue" after the first.
- With photos: "N of M added" (M is the ad's limit from the server), the hint "Hold and drag a photo to change the order. First photo = cover.", a grid of 4:5 tiles (3 across from 768 px, 2 on a phone) and a last "Add more" tile. Each photo is shown whole over a blurred copy of itself; the first has a "Cover" badge.
- Under each photo one row of icon buttons: make cover (not on the cover), left, right, bin on the right (red). Every button keeps its aria-label and title. They are 44 px high; the four share the row, so they are 36 to 44 px wide (four 44 px buttons cannot fit in a two-column phone tile; the design file overflows there too).
- Drag to reorder (Pointer Events, no library): a mouse drags after 5 px; a finger after 250 ms held still (a finger that moves first is scrolling). The lifted photo tilts and floats with a "Moving..." chip, its old place is a dashed pink slot that follows the pointer, a drop saves the order and the first photo becomes the cover. While a photo is lifted the page does not scroll under a finger. The arrow buttons do the same job without dragging.
- States: uploading ("Uploading N%" and a bar over the photo), failed (dark overlay "Could not upload", Try again when a retry makes sense, the bin under the tile, the reason line). A file that is not a photo shows a neutral placeholder, never a broken image.
- Guidelines panel (`PhotoGuidelines.tsx`): three example tiles (Clear, real / Text on photo / Blurry), Do list, Don't list, File rules box and the closing line. The numbers in the file rules come from the upload rules (formats, 60 seconds, 100 MB, the ad's photo and video limit), not from the text. Side column on a computer, under the grid on a phone.
- Live preview on this step: the details card with the cover as its photo, outlined, with a "Cover" tag; on a phone the same card opens from the Preview button.
- Step title "Photos", lead "The first photo is your cover." (one line for every category).
- Support bubble visible on this step (tested).
- New text keys `post.v2.ph.*` only. Pure helpers `photo-order.ts` with unit tests (`tests/server/ui/photo-order.test.ts`).
- STATUS.md: H21.2 approved, H21.3 row added.
