# H17.6 Ad images

Branch `haiku/h17.6-ad-images`.

## Done
- Every ad photo has the alt text "{ad title}, {area or city}, photo N" (`adPhotoAlt` in `src/lib/ad-photo-alt.ts`): the card photos on Home and in every list (including similar ads and the 404 page), the old-style card photo, the pop-out photo, and the main photo on the ad page. Photo numbers count photos only (a video slide takes no number). The blurred copy behind the ad page photo stays `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`. The small thumbnails stay `alt=""`, as the button around each has its own label.
- Every ad image has `width` and `height` (card photos 400x500, ad page photo 960x1200, thumbnails 120x120, chat mini card 56x56); CSS sizing is unchanged.
- Only the first photo of the first four cards of a list loads at once (`FIRST_ROW_CARDS`); every other card photo is lazy. Fallback ads, similar ads and the other lists below the fold are lazy.
