# H14.2 part 2: the whole ad card is the link in the user panel

Pattern as in part 1: one real link stretched over the card, other controls above it, pointer cursor, card focus ring, Tab then Enter.

## Done
- The tile card (`AdCard`, used by My ads on the account home and on the My ads board, Saved ads (rail and page), Recently viewed): its body link is stretched over the whole card (`::after`, inset 0, in `globals.css`); the photo arrows, contact circles and owner buttons (Edit, Boost, Bump, Pause, Delete) sit above it; the status row and the empty space open the ad; the card shows a ring when its link has keyboard focus. One click event per open (the existing counter counts the click on the card link; owner views never count).
- Analytics table: the title is a link stretched over its row (`up-row-link`), the row has a pointer cursor and a ring on focus. Drafts open their editor.
- No "View ad" or "View my ads" text existed on these panel components (the buttons that Opus listed were already links on the card); the specs assert none appears.

## Not in this PR
ListingCards, SimilarAds, NotFoundView and FreshAds wait for H14.1 (they use the row card or the tile; the tile change above also applies to FreshAds while it still uses the tile).
