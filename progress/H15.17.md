# H15.17 (FIX-17): h9-6 account consistency fails on the messages page

The test (h9-6-account-consistency.e2e.ts:72) failed 3 of 3 on a production build of main. It checks every account page for small text, small targets, panel shape and axe, at 1440 and then 390. There were TWO causes, one in the product and one in an old expectation.

1. PRODUCT (messages page, 1440 and 390): the small labels that sit on ad photos were under 12 px, below the site rule of 12 px for text: the photo count ("4") and the "tharkoo.com" mark on the ad cards (AdCard: `.tkn-ls-count` 11 px, `.tkn-ls-wm` 10 px, the tier tag `.tkn-ls-tier` 10 px) and on the row cards (`.tk-row-count` 11 px, `.tk-row-mark` 10 px, the tag above it 10 px). The page shows both kinds of card. All six are now 12 px (listing-spec.css and listing-grid.css). The test found 14 small texts on the old build.
2. STALE EXPECTATION (alerts page, 390): the test wants every panel to have 22 px padding, but H12.3 made the alerts form card 14 px on a phone on purpose, so its inputs fill 90 percent or more of the card (tested by the H12.3 spec). Both are right. The test now expects 14 px for the `up-panel-tight` card at 820 px wide or less and 22 px for every other panel; nothing else in the test was loosened.

Result on a production build of main with the fix: h9-6-account-consistency passes (it failed before with 14 small texts on the messages page, then on the alerts panel); a32-listing, post-steps-3-4 and the other card specs run with it pass (17 passed).

Not part of this card: tests/e2e/home-ad-cards.e2e.ts:52 ("pills are category and city only") also fails on main since H16.5, because the Sample ad pill is a third pill on sample cards. It is told to the coordinator; H17.1 (sample-ad honesty) changes these cards and will update that test.
