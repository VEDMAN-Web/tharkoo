# H17.10: sample ad text that fits each category, and "Refresh sample ad text" (Opus #419)

## What changed
- New templates in `src/server/listings/demo-text.ts`, found by the category's address (slug), never by its name, so a rename in the admin changes nothing here:
  - `dating` (Friends & Dating): friendship and dating introductions, no age or body focus, nothing about money: coffee, walks, book lovers, quiz night, cooking, music, a food walk, board games, day trips (10 templates).
  - `transgender` (LGBTQ+ Community): community meetups, a peer support circle, pride planning, art and poetry, film nights, friendship groups, a community kitchen, an allies book club, board games, a storytelling workshop (10).
  - `spa` (Spa & Salon): salon, day spa, beauty and grooming only: haircut and styling, facial and skin care, manicure and pedicure, bridal makeup, men's grooming, a day spa package (facial, hair spa, foot soak), hair spa and colour, threading and waxing, party makeup, kids haircuts (10). No body massage wording (tested).
  - `personal-ads` (Meet People): hobby, sport and activity partners: badminton, a morning run group, chess, cycling, a photography walk, trekking, cricket, a guitar jam, yoga in the park, table tennis (10).
  - Each template names the city, and about half name an AREA of the city. The area is the ad's own area, or one of the city's known areas picked by the ad number; with none, only the templates without an area are used. The same ad always gets the same words.
- Admin action "Refresh sample ad text..." on Admin > All ads > Demo ads (admins, same permission as ending demo ads): `refreshDemoText` rewrites only the title and text of SAMPLE ads (owned by the seed account, address demo-N) in those four categories. A typed confirmation `REFRESH <number of sample ads in those categories>` is needed, the number is shown in the dialog; one audit entry ("Rewrote the text of N sample ads", with the actor's role); Undo (new undo kind `demo.text`, from the toast or History) puts every old title and text back and leaves an ad that was edited by hand since alone. It never touches a real ad, a price, a photo, an address or the status, and makes no change when the text is already up to date ("already up to date"). Nothing is run against any live database by this PR: Opus presses it on the live admin after the merge.
- Code defaults that contradicted the new names are replaced: the Home card fallback for the `wellness` category no longer says "therapeutic massage", the `personal-ads` fallback and seed description now say hobby, sport and activity partners, and the seed's demo lines for `personal-ads` are hobby partners (so a fresh seed matches). Category names and descriptions on the live site come from the admin and are not changed here.
