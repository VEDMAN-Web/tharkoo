# H19.6b - Post an ad, rebuilt (PART B of 2)

Stacked on part A (PR #497, branch haiku/h19.6-post-ad). Opens after #497 merges.

## Card items in this PR (B)
6. No date of birth in the wizard. "I am 18 or over" is asked once, on the last step only (the Review step's existing tick). The 18+ age on the ad, the no-video rule for people categories and the publish gate are untouched.
7. Contact step: the Phone and email / Phone only / Email only buttons are gone (emails are never shown on ads, contact is always phone). Phone is required. WhatsApp and Telegram are switches; Telegram on shows its username box right under the switch. "Name on your ads" and the city come after the contact fields.
8. Last step: the "This ad is FREE (free ad N of M)" box is replaced by a Free / TOP / Super Top choice. Each has an (i) tooltip with what it does and the price in ad credits. Publishing with TOP or Super Top takes the plan price in ad credits (together with the posting fee when posting is paid) and starts the boost at once. Not enough ad credits: openRecharge({ wallet: "ad" }) opens the ad-credit sheet and the publish carries on after the credits are added. src/components/recharge/** is not touched.
9. "Your ad is live" screen: shorter, one column, buttons View your ad / My ads / Post another ad. The Promote pop-up uses the same plan cards (with (i)) as step 8 (new shared PlanChoice) and recharges ad credits.
Also: the 70/30 call note ("Users pay N credits per call. You keep M credits (Rs X). Tharkoo keeps the rest." with its tooltip) is shown on the last step by reusing the H19.10 EarnLine component (exported from TourInbox.tsx, not copied).

## Notes
- An ad held for review is not boosted (the server only promotes live ads): the success line says to boost it from My ads once it is live, and nothing is charged.
- If the boost purchase fails after the ad is published, the ad stays live, the success screen says the boost was not bought and why. No credits are taken.
- The boost purchase uses a fixed idempotency key per ad and plan, so a double click is one purchase.
- Left in the code and unchanged: the three-step form (New design off) still has its own contact buttons.
- NEED (small): the 70/30 line uses GET /api/tours/earn-info, which lists the prices of the categories of the vendor's LIVE ads, so a vendor with no live ad yet sees no line. To show it for the category being posted, earn-info needs an optional categoryId (src/server/tours/earnings.ts and the tours route are not on this card's list).
- NEED (small): src/components/vendor/TourInbox.tsx is not on the card's file list; only three words changed there (export EarnInfo and EarnLine).
- Pre-existing, not touched: the axe label-content-name-mismatch on the header coin chip.
