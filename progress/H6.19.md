## Done

1. **Post an ad, five steps** (`src/components/PostWizard.tsx`, `src/components/post/PostFields.tsx`, `src/components/post/PostPreview.tsx`).
   - Category, Details, Photos & videos, Contact, Review. Progress rail with the current and finished steps.
   - Category tiles and sub-category chips (buttons with `aria-pressed`).
   - Live preview card that updates as the user types (`PostPreview`, not the public card, so it never counts a view).
   - Saves: the draft is created at the end of Details (as before); contact is saved at the end of Contact; publish is at Review.
   - Review: the 72-hour note and the 18+ tick. The terms and the 18+ tick are checked on the Review step.
   - Default step titles changed in `src/components/post/copy.ts` (Category, Details, Photos & videos, Contact, Review). The layout editor still overrides them.
2. **Photos and videos**: unchanged server rules; the "N of 10 photos and videos" counter, Cover, Make cover and Remove are the H6.18 ones.
3. **Vendor panel** (`src/components/vendor/VendorScreens.tsx`, `VendorShell.tsx`, `OwnAds.tsx`).
   - Welcome strip, four numbers (Live ads, Views and Clicks all time, Coins), My ads, wallet card, chats link, profile progress (three checks from data), TourInbox, security tips.
   - Phone bottom bar with a big + (`.tk-vbar`, shown under 820 px).
   - "Your ads": tabs All, Live, In review, Ended with counts; "Load more" (12 per page); status label (Live, Ending soon, In review, Ended, Draft, Paused, Rejected); owner actions Edit, Renew, Promote, Post again, and a More menu with Pause or Resume and Delete.
4. **Shared listing card** (`src/components/ui/AdCard.tsx`): new `owner`, `ownerStatus`, `ownerActions` props. An owner card never records the owner's own views or clicks (H6.17). The vendor panel uses the same card as the public pages.
5. **Server**
   - `GET /api/listings` now returns card data (`card`, built with the same `presentRows` the public pages use) plus `counts` and `totals` (all-time shown views and clicks, from the existing `shownCountsFor`, capped at 500 ads). New `group` query (`live`, `review`, `ended`). Response has `Cache-Control: private, max-age=10`.
   - Files: `src/server/listings/owner-cards.ts`, `src/server/listings/owner-groups.ts`, `src/server/listings/service.ts` (`listOwn` uses the shared filter), `src/server/shared/schemas/listings.ts`, `src/app/api/listings/[[...slug]]/route.ts`.
6. **Glass look** (`src/app/globals.css`, appended block). Tokens scoped to `.tk-glass-page`. Panels at 72 percent with blur(18px) saturate(170%); fallbacks for no backdrop-filter and for prefers-reduced-transparency; reduced motion turns transitions off; 44 px targets on the panel and post screens.
7. **Tests**
   - Unit: `tests/server/ui/ad-status.test.ts`, `tests/server/listings/owner-groups.test.ts`, `tests/server/listings/owner-tab-counts.test.ts`. Updated `tests/server/layout/post.test.ts` (photo title).
   - e2e: new `tests/e2e/post-insert-panel.e2e.ts` (post end to end; the vendor home shows the shared card with status and owner actions; five steps in order). Updated `post-check-switch`, `post-media`, `advertiser`, `flows` (shared helper) and `h6-features` to the five steps and the new tab and card markup.

## Not done

- "Chat online now" and "Video call and verify" toggles: no stored field exists for either, so they are not built. Contact keeps Phone, WhatsApp and Telegram.
- The global site header is not restyled to the 65 percent glass; the glass applies to the page background and panels of the post and vendor screens.
- Views and clicks on the welcome numbers are all time, not last 7 days (the counts store has no per-day figure).
- Chats card is a link with the unread badge; no message preview.
- "Ended" is expired and archived. Paused and rejected ads appear under All only.
