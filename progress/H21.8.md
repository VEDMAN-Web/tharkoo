# H21.8 - remove the ad price everywhere

Ads no longer show or collect a price. The `price` field stays in the database (old ads keep their value, nothing is deleted). Credit, pack and plan prices are untouched.

## Done
- Ad cards: list row, popout, grid card (`AdCard`), small card (`MiniAdCard`, also in the chat inbox), alternatives in the ad popup: no price, no "Price on request". Dead `PriceTag.tsx`, `price.ts`, the text key `ad.price.request` and the price CSS rules are gone.
- Ad page (`NewAdDetail`): no price line in the contact card or under the title on phones; no `priceUnit` prop.
- Discover API: the `minPrice` / `maxPrice` filter is gone (there is no price sort or filter UI left).
- Admin: the Price box is gone from the ad edit form and from the create-ad form. The edit form keeps the stored value untouched when saving.
- Structured data (`src/lib/seo/schema.ts`): no `offers`, no Offer, no price, no priceCurrency on Product, RealEstateListing or Service. A Product now needs only name and description. The admin SEO preview no longer passes a price. The city/category landing text (`landing.ts`) no longer says anything about prices.

## Tests changed on purpose (old rules said the price must show or be in the Offer)
- Unit: `tests/server/ui/card-price.test.ts`, `tests/server/ui/seo-schema.test.ts`, `tests/server/ui/seo-delta.test.ts`, `tests/server/seo/h16-4-ad-schema.test.ts`, `tests/server/seo/settings.test.ts`, `tests/server/listings/h16-4-ad-seo.test.ts`, `tests/server/search/price.test.ts` (the display formatter is gone; the server `cleanPrice` check stays).
- E2E: `h14-1-listing-rows` (card has no price), `ad-page-content` (no price line, no "Price on request"), `a33-ad-edit` (edits the description instead of the price; the stored price stays 20000).
