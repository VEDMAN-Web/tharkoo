# H6.16: post-ad photo step and "My photos & videos" (relay #58, lane F)

## Done
- Post-ad photo step (`src/components/post/PhotoStep.tsx`): several photos at once, thumbnail grid with upload progress, remove, make cover, move left/right, "N of M photos" against the ad's limit. Videos show a preview frame with controls (play) and remove.
- `/u/account/media/` is "My photos & videos": newest first, All / Photos / Videos filters, each item with thumbnail, ad link, status (Live, Being checked, Refused with reason), date and size. Remove detaches the item from its ad and closes it. A live ad's cover is refused.
- Owner only: every read and write is scoped to the signed-in user's id on the server. ID documents are never listed (kind filter).
- Vendor dashboard tile "Photos & videos" links to the library.
- Audit row `media.remove` on each remove (plus the existing `media.delete`).
- Attaching a photo that is still being checked (`scanning`) is allowed; publishing still needs it cleared.

- Refused uploads that never attached to an ad show in the library only; the wizard grid shows attached photos.
