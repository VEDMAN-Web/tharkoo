# H19.3 Video call flow for users

Branch `haiku/h19.3-video-call-flow` (from main with H19.2 and H19.10).

## Done
1. The "Video call and verify" pop-up (`AdPopup.tsx`): one headline, three benefit lines (See the person live before you meet / Verify before you spend thousands / You pay only if the call connects), the price "1 call = N credits" (N is the category price, the lowest is 10 after H19.10), the user's call-credit balance, and ONE main button. The old two tiles and the "Chat first" button are gone; "Chat with {name} first" stays as a small text link, not a button.
2. Not enough call credits: the main button reads "Buy call credits" and the same pop-up switches to the buy step (`data-step="buy"`): the new `CallPacks` (call packs only, four of them, plus an own-amount box, card or crypto, one Pay button). No tabs, no ad credits, no "free ads", no "per ad", no "publish" in the pop-up or its keys (a test scans the keys).
3. The way back: the pop-up starts the payment with `returnTo = <this ad page>?call=1`. After a PAID payment the checkout sends the person back with `&paid=1&added=N` (`callBackUrl`, `back-url.ts`); a failed or cancelled one never does. `ContactButtons` reads `?call=1` once (like the existing `?chat=1`), reopens the pop-up on the call step, shows "Recharge successful, N call credits added" and the "Start video call" button, and removes the flags from the address. The global "credits added" toast is skipped for that return so the message is not shown twice.
4. Header credit chip: hover or keyboard focus (desktop) and tap (phone) show "Call credits: N · Ad credits: N" in a small tooltip, with a "Buy credits" button that opens the recharge sheet (the chip used to open it on click). The chip is now shown on a phone too (it was hidden under 600 px; the balances were only in the account menu).
