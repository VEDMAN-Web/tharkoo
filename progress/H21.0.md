# H21.0 — post-ad redesign: shared shell

## Done
- New shell around every screen of the post-ad flow (`src/components/post/PostShell.tsx`, `post-shell.css`):
  - Own header: phone shows an exit arrow, "Post an ad" and the ad-credit pill (wallet icon, balance, +); computer shows the logo, "Post an ad" in the middle, the Saved chip, "Save draft and exit" and the pill with "ad credits". The pill shows the live ad balance (useAccount) and opens Buy credits for ad credits only.
  - 3 px progress line, gradient #f06aa8 to #c2126f with a glow, animated 0.4 s. Widths 16/36/56/76/94 percent per step and 100 percent on the Done screens (`progressPercent` in post-form.ts, unit tested). Not shown on Verify ID.
  - Steps rail on a computer (232 px card, "Your ad" label, done steps are green ticks and clickable, current step pink, "Need help?" at the bottom). Phone shows the step strip "Step N of M · Title" and a sticky bottom bar (Back icon button, then the primary button).
  - The support bubble is the existing chat bubble, moved to 28 px from the edges on a computer and 16 right / 96 bottom on a phone.
  - The site header and footer are hidden on this flow (CSS `body:has(.tkp-shell)`), so there is one header.
  - Only the page scrolls: the step card, side column, summary, preview sheet and textareas no longer have inner scroll boxes or fit-the-screen heights. Inputs are 52 px / 16 px text; tap targets are 44 px.
- Draft saving was already on every Continue and field blur (unchanged); `?step=` restore unchanged.
- Verify ID and the Done screens use the same shell.
- New text keys `post.v2.*` only.
- STATUS.md: H20.1 approved (#501 merged), H21.0 row added.
