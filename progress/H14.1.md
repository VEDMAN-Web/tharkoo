# H14.1 Listing cards in the approved design (part 1: rows and slider)

Branch: haiku/h14.1-approved-listing-cards (off main fff42d8). Opus #370, #371, #372.

## Done (part 1)
- New `article.tk-row` card (`ListingRow.tsx`) with the approved values: row, 200 px photo at 4:5, radius 16, body padding 18/20, title 18/700 (two lines), 38 px save heart, description 14 px (two lines), footer with the price 17/800 (only when the ad has one), then the tags (Photo verified only when true, category, "City, Area"), and views and clicks 13 px with eye and hand icons on the right.
- Badge top left from the real plan (variant): New, Top, SUPER Top, Featured (star and highlight), gradient #B45309 to #92400E. Media count pill (photos plus video) bottom left, "tharkoo.com" mark bottom right, slider dots, Online badge (top right of the photo).
- Later changes from Vedant (Opus #374, #375, #378): the whole card is the link (title link stretched with ::after, heart and slider buttons above it, the photo track above it so it can be swiped, one click counted per open); views and clicks read "1 view", "5 views", "1 click", "5 clicks" (one number, one word); no "Price on request" text on the row; the tags (Photo verified, category, place) sit in the footer after the price, views and clicks on the right; on a phone the footer wraps (price and tags on line 1, views and clicks on line 2) and nothing is cut. People ads (dating, friendship, meetups, personal ads, or an ad with an age) have no price, so the footer's left side is just the tags: intentional, not a missing value.
- Photo slider and video are the Home card's code, not a copy: `use-card-slider.ts` (hook) and `CardSlides.tsx` (track, arrows, dots) are now used by `HomeAdCard` and `ListingRow`. Home markup is unchanged (home baseline snapshot test passes).
- Views and clicks are the real counters (`useShownCounts`, /api/views/counts, the same as the ad page). A number is shown only after the counts have loaded (hidden before that, never a made-up 0); a real 0 shows 0.
- Used by: category, city, district and tag listing pages, search, Similar ads and More ads you may like (carousel, two across at 1440, one on smaller screens), the 404 latest ads, Chats "More ads you may like", account Fresh ads. Home is unchanged.
- People ads (an ad with an age) never play video on a card and do not show a price (wellness keeps it), the same rule as the ad page.
- Phone (600 px and below): the row stacks, photo full width at 4:3, 72 px of room under the list for the chat button.
- Words: new keys `card.photoVerified`, `card.clicks`, `card.clickOne`; tier words reuse existing keys.
