# H7.91: sticky, instant filter column and phone sheet (part 2)

## Done
- Desktop (1024 px and up): the filter column on every listing page is sticky (it stays under the header and scrolls inside itself), compact (40 px selects: Category alone, State and City side by side, Area alone; quick filters as small toggle chips; the category's own filters as collapsed accordions) and applies a change at once. Its sticky footer has Clear all and Show N ads with the live count.
- Phone and tablet (under 1024 px): no column. A sticky bar under the header holds Filters (n), and Sort on the search page; Filters opens a full-height bottom sheet with the same controls and a sticky Show N ads button; the active chips row stays above the results.
