# H7.91: fixed filter column, shorter cards, photo arrows, card video

## Done
- Desktop (1024 px and up): the filter column on /search/ and every listing page (category, city, state, district, tag) is a fixed-size block that never scrolls. Width 272 px, padding 14, gaps 8, controls 40 px, toggles 36 px. Top to bottom: Filters with a count badge and Clear all (only when something is on), keyword (search page), Category, State and City side by side, Area only once a city with areas is chosen, the toggles in a 2 by 2 grid, the category's own filters as accordions (closed, one open at a time, count on a closed one, an open panel scrolls inside itself when the window is short). Changes apply at once. The column is at most 500 px tall at 1366x768, 1536x730, 1536x674, 1440x900 and 1920x1080.
- Phone and tablet (under 1024 px): sticky Filters (n) bar, full-height sheet with the same blocks at 44 px and a Show N ads button.
- List cards: photo 200 by 216 on desktop (236 high on a phone), padding 16. Prev and next arrows (32 px circles) on hover and always on touch screens, swipe on the photo, the photo itself opens the ad. A card with a video shows a play badge; the muted video plays while the mouse is on the card, and on a touch screen while the card is at least 60% on screen, one at a time, never with reduced motion.
- Views are counted again on the new-design ad page (the view counter was only mounted on the old design). Public cards and the ad page show no views or clicks; the owner dashboard still does.
- ad-counts e2e settled: stale card link selector, admin pick of a published ad, view leak between tests; the admin 401 was the local server missing the admin bootstrap login (not an app bug).
