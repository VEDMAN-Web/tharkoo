# H17.3 Crawl paths

Branch `haiku/h17.3-crawl-paths`.

## Done
- Page 2 and later of every list (category, city or state, area, sub-category, tag) say so: " (page N)" is added to the title (before the site name) and to the heading, through `pagedText` in `src/lib/seo/pagination.ts`. The canonical of a later page was already its own address (`?page=N`) and stays so; a later page stays noindex, follow as before.
- The numbered page links (1 2 3 ... Next) were already real anchors (`<a href="?page=N">`, rel prev and next); a test now locks that in with the server-rendered markup.
- Ad pages have a plain link "More ads in {city}" to the city page (`/cities/<city>/`), under the similar ads (UI text key `ad.moreInCity`, editable on Site text).
- `/search/` is noindex, follow whatever the site-wide switch says (explicit `follow: true`, test with the site live). Tag pages were already `index: false, follow: true`.

## Tests
- `tests/server/seo/h17-3-crawl-paths.test.ts`: pagedText, pager anchors, More link, search robots with the site live.
- `tests/e2e/h17-3-crawl-paths.e2e.ts`: pager anchors on a real list, page 2 title/heading/canonical, the link on an ad page (shots at 390, 768, 1440 in `.shots/H17.3/`), search noindex (the e2e server runs with the site noindex, so the follow part is covered by the unit test).
