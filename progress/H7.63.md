# H7.63: listing pagination, 15 ads per page

## Done
- Every listing feed takes 15 own ads per page (LISTING_PAGE_SIZE): category, state, city, district, sub-category hub, tag, search and the city page "Latest ads".
- One shared pager (ListingPager, listing-pager.ts, listing-pager.css) replaces the "Show more" link on all of them. Desktop and tablet: Previous, numbers with gaps, Next; the current page is filled pink with aria-current; Previous on page 1 and Next on the last page are disabled spans. Phone under 600 px: Prev, "Page N of M", Next, no number row. "Showing a-b of N ads" above it. Every button is a real link to a clean ?page=N address (page 1 has no ?page), filters kept.
- Clicking a page scrolls to the top of the results.
- Fallback ads only on the last page, and only up to 15 cards on that page.
- rel prev and next in the head (Metadata.pagination) on category, state, city, district, sub-category hub, tag and city pages when no filter is set. Canonicals unchanged (keep ?page=N for N above 1). A page beyond the last is a 404 (the tag page and the category page now check it).
- Pager words are UI-text keys (listing.pager.*).

## Not done
- /search/ has no rel prev and next (it is always noindex and driven by query terms).
- Page 1 of the category page still shows the "star" ads above the 15 feed ads.
