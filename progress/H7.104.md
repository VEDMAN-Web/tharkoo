# H7.104: Home grid, auto-swipe cards, one listing template

## Part 1 (this PR): Home and auto-swipe
- Home categories block shows the first N categories by admin order; N is a setting on the Home page editor ("Number of categories on Home"), default 4, 1 to 8.
- Home ads: a grid, 4 columns by 2 rows from 1024 px, 2 by 4 under it; the section arrows are gone; "See all ads" stays. Photo about 285 x 300, title 16 px, pills 12 px.
- Auto-swipe on the Home card and its hover pop-out: video first (muted, to its end, at most 15 s), then each photo 3 s, then loop; runs only while at least 50% of the card is on screen and the tab is visible; each card starts offset by its place x 600 ms; a click or swipe pauses it 6 s; reduced motion: no auto move; data saver: no video; at most 4 videos playing on a computer and 1 on a phone.
