# H8.32 part 2 of 2: listing blocks and UI-text words

## Done
- The category page now draws the `relatedCategories` ("Popular searches": the public tag links of the category) and `nearbyCities` ("Nearby": cities that have ads in this category) blocks of the listing layout, in the layout's order, with the block's own title and "most links" setting. Before, the category page skipped them. The filter panel stays in the title band and the area links belong to city pages, so those two stay skipped.
- Empty-state and fallback words are UI text keys (editable in Menus & site text): `listing.fallback.nearby`, `.category`, `.latest` (the three "more ads" titles) and `listing.empty.leadPlace`, `buttonPlace`, `leadSearch`, `buttonPost`, `leadTag`, `buttonCategory`, `leadIndia`, `buttonIndia`. The seven listing pages that had these sentences in code (category, city or state, district, sub-category, tag, /cities/<city>/, search) now read them through `t()`; the built-in words are identical, so nothing changes until someone edits a key.
- `loadFallbackSections` takes the page's `t` (optional; without it the old words).

## Not done

- The category page's intro band and the list of cities still use their own words; only the two blocks and the empty and fallback words moved.
