# Placeholder photos

All images in this folder are AI-generated illustrations of fictional adult women, created for Tharkoo on 11 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta).

No real persons are depicted. The faces and figures are not based on, and are not intended to resemble, any identifiable living person, public figure, or existing photograph.

The images are fashion-editorial and glamour portraits of fictional adults clearly 25 or older, fully clothed, for use as site placeholders (hero, category cards, listing and profile portraits). They are not photographs of real models and are not accompanied by a model release.

| File | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `hero.webp` | Fictional adult woman in a modest rose-plum evening gown on a rooftop at dusk. Cropped to 960×880 for the homepage hero. |
| `dating/01.webp` | Fictional adult woman seated at a candlelit restaurant table, modest rose midi dress. Homepage dating category card. |
| `friendship/01.webp` | Two fictional adult women laughing together at a sunlit cafe. Homepage friendship category card. |
| `wellness/01.webp` | Fictional adult woman in modest full-coverage athletic wear in a yoga studio. Homepage wellness category card. |
| `meetups/01.webp` | Fictional adult woman in a modest plum midi dress at a rooftop evening gathering. Homepage meetups category card. |
| `personal-ads/01.webp` | Fictional adult woman in a modest plum and rose silk saree, studio portrait. Homepage personal-ads category card. |
| `profiles/01.webp` … `profiles/12.webp` | Twelve fictional adult fashion-editorial portraits (4:5) for listing cards and ad galleries. Mix of Indian and international looks, including saree, lehenga, anarkali, evening dresses and smart-casual outfits. |

Matching `*-blur.webp` files are tiny low-resolution thumbs generated from the same images, for use as blur placeholders.

These files are project-original AI generations for Tharkoo and replace earlier Unsplash object-and-place photographs in the corresponding hero and featured-category `01.webp` paths.
